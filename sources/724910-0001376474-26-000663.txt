NVE Corporation Reports Second Quarter Results and Announces Quarterly Dividend
EDEN PRAIRIE, Minn.—October 21, 2026—NVE Corporation (Nasdaq: NVEC) announced financial results today for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Total revenue for the second quarter of fiscal 2027 decreased 8% to $5.83 million from $6.35 million for the prior-year quarter. The decrease was due to a 9% decrease in product sales, partially offset by a 25% increase in contract research and development revenue. Net income for the second quarter of fiscal 2027 decreased 4% to $3.17 million, or $0.65 per diluted share, compared to $3.31 million, or $0.68 per share, for the prior-year quarter.
For the first six months of fiscal 2027, total revenue increased 36% to $16.9 million from $12.5 million for the first six months of the prior year. The increase was due to a 35% increase in product sales and a 38% increase in contract research and development revenue. Net income increased 39% to $9.57 million, or $1.97 per diluted share, from $6.89 million, or $1.42 per share, for the first half of fiscal 2026.
The company also announced a quarterly cash dividend to shareholders of $1.00 per share of common stock, payable November 30, 2026 to shareholders of record as of November 2, 2026.
“We’re pleased to report a 36% increase in revenue for the six months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the prior-year period, despite an 8% decrease in revenue for the second quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to the prior-year quarter,” said NVE President and Chief Executive Officer Peter G. Eames, PhD.
NVE is a leader in the practical commercialization of spintronics, a nanotechnology that relies on electron spin rather than electron charge to acquire, store, and transmit information. The company manufactures high-performance spintronic products including sensors and couplers that are used to acquire and transmit data.
