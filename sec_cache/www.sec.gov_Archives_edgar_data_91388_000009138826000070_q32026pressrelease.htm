Smithfield Foods Reports Third Quarter Results
•Packaged Meats Delivers Higher Q3 Operating Profit YoY; on Track for Fifth Consecutive Year Above $1 Billion in Operating Profit
•Company Completes Nathan’s Famous Acquisition, Bolstering its Packaged Meats Brand Portfolio
•Company Updates FY 2026 Outlook to Reflect Strong Packaged Meats Performance and Softer Commodity Markets
•Strong Balance Sheet and Improved Cash Generation Support Continued Investment and Capital Allocation Priorities

SMITHFIELD, Va., October 27, 2026 -- Smithfield Foods, Inc. (Nasdaq: SFD), an American food company and an industry leader in value-added packaged meats and fresh pork, today reported results for its fiscal 2026 third quarter ended September 27, 2026.
Third Quarter Fiscal 2026 Financial Highlights
•Sales of $3.4 billion, down 8.3% from the third quarter of fiscal 2025
•Operating profit of $127 million, compared to $310 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025; adjusted operating profit(1) of $145 million, compared to $310 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025
•Operating profit margin of 3.7%, compared to 8.3% in the third quarter of fiscal 2025; adjusted operating profit margin(1) of 4.2%, compared to 8.3% in the third quarter of fiscal 2025
•Packaged Meats operating profit of $230 million, compared to $226 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2025
•Packaged Meats operating profit margin of 11.6%, compared to 10.8% in the third quarter of fiscal 2025
•Diluted earnings per share of $0.23, compared to $0.63 in the third quarter of fiscal 2025; adjusted diluted earnings per share(1) of $0.26, compared to $0.58 in the third quarter of fiscal 2025
First Nine Months Fiscal 2026 Financial Highlights
•Sales of $10.9 billion, down 3.2% from the first nine months of fiscal 2025
•Operating profit of $750 million, compared to $892 million for the first nine months of fiscal 2025; adjusted operating profit(1) of $783 million, compared to $934 million for the first nine months of fiscal 2025
•Operating profit margin of 6.9%, compared to 7.9% for the first nine months of fiscal 2025; adjusted operating profit margin(1) of 7.2%, compared to 8.3% for the first nine months of fiscal 2025
•Packaged Meats operating profit of $770 million, compared to $792 million for the first nine months of fiscal 2025; Packaged Meats adjusted operating profit(1) of $770 million, compared to $787 million for the first nine months of fiscal 2025
•Packaged Meats operating profit margin of 12.5%, compared to 12.8% for the first nine months of fiscal 2025; Packaged Meats adjusted operating profit margin(1) of 12.5%, compared to 12.7% for the first nine months of fiscal 2025
•Diluted earnings per share of $1.45, compared to $1.68 for the first nine months of fiscal 2025; adjusted diluted earnings per share(1) of $1.52, compared to $1.72 for the first nine months of fiscal 2025
•Net cash flows from operating activities of $365 million, up $244 million from the first nine months of fiscal 2025

CEO Perspective

“We reported third quarter adjusted operating profit within the range we provided in September, led by strong results in our cornerstone Packaged Meats segment, with our upstream segments challenged by lower pork commodity markets,” said Smithfield President and CEO Shane Smith. “Packaged Meats remains on track to deliver its fifth consecutive year above $1 billion in operating profit in fiscal 2026. The completion of the Nathan’s Famous acquisition further strengthens our brand portfolio and expands our opportunities for profitable growth.”

Smith added, “While we expect commodity market pressure in our upstream businesses to continue in the fourth quarter, our teams remain focused on navigating this environment with operational discipline. We have a strong balance sheet and solid cash generation, giving us the financial flexibility to support our dividend and reinvest in the business, driving shareholder value and positioning Smithfield for long-term growth.”

Dividend Update

On April 21, 2026, May 28, 2026, and August 27, 2026, we paid dividends of $0.3125 per share to shareholders. We anticipate a remaining quarterly dividend for fiscal year 2026 of $0.3125 per share, resulting in an annual dividend rate of $1.25 per share. The declaration of dividends is subject to the discretion of our Board and depends on various factors, including our net income, financial condition, cash requirements, business prospects, and other factors that our Board deems relevant to its analysis and decision making.

FY 2026 Outlook

As we look to the remainder of 2026, our outlook takes into account current macroeconomic challenges including softer fresh pork and hog commodity markets.

The Company’s financial outlook is as follows:

•Total Company sales to be down low-single-digits compared to fiscal year 2025

•Packaged Meats segment adjusted operating profit ranging from $1,090 million to $1,140 million

•Fresh Pork segment adjusted operating results ranging from a loss of $50 million to breakeven

•Hog Production segment adjusted operating results ranging from a loss of $25 million to a profit of $25 million

•Total Company adjusted operating profit ranging from $900 million to $1,000 million

•Capital expenditures of between $350 million and $375 million. Our capital expenditures outlook includes investments in profit improvement projects as well as projects for maintenance and repair, and excludes investments in our proposed Sioux Falls processing facility

•An effective tax rate of between 22.5% and 24.5%

The Company’s outlook for 2026 includes 53 weeks of results and approximately two months of results for Nathan’s Famous following the October 26, 2026 acquisition close, and excludes the impact of the investment in the new processing facility in Sioux Falls, South Dakota.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

This press release includes certain financial information that is not presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”), including (1) adjusted net income attributable to Smithfield, (2) adjusted net income per diluted common share attributable to Smithfield, (3) EBITDA, (4) adjusted EBITDA, (5) adjusted EBITDA margin, (6) adjusted operating profit, (7) adjusted operating profit margin, (8) net debt and (9) ratio of net debt to adjusted EBITDA. We refer to these measures as “non-GAAP” financial measures.

(1) Adjusted net income attributable to Smithfield is defined as net income, excluding the effects of transactions or events that are not part of our core business activities or are unusual in nature (whether gains or losses) and the tax effects of the foregoing items. We believe that adjusted net income attributable to Smithfield is a useful measure because it excludes the effects of items that are unusual in nature, infrequent in occurrence or otherwise stem from strategic decisions to restructure our operations. (2) Adjusted net income per diluted common share attributable to Smithfield is defined as adjusted net income attributable to Smithfield divided by diluted weighted average shares outstanding. (3) EBITDA is defined as earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. We believe that EBITDA is a useful measure because it excludes the effects of financing and investing activities by eliminating interest and depreciation costs to provide a comparable year-over-year analysis. (4) Adjusted EBITDA is defined as EBITDA further adjusted for the effects of items that are unusual in nature, infrequent in occurrence or otherwise stem from strategic decisions to restructure our operations. We believe that adjusted EBITDA is a useful measure because it excludes the effects of items that are unusual in nature, infrequent in occurrence or otherwise stem from strategic decisions to restructure our operations. (5) Adjusted EBITDA margin is defined as adjusted EBITDA divided by total sales. We believe that adjusted EBITDA margin is a useful measure because it evaluates overall operating performance, ability to pursue and service possible debt opportunities and possible future investment opportunities. (6) Adjusted operating profit is defined as operating profit, excluding the effects of items that are unusual in nature, infrequent in occurrence or otherwise stem from strategic decisions to restructure our operations. (7) Adjusted operating profit margin is adjusted operating profit expressed as a percentage of sales. We believe that adjusted net income per diluted common share attributable to Smithfield, adjusted operating profit and adjusted operating profit margin provide a better understanding of underlying operating results and trends of established, ongoing operations of our business. (8) Net debt is defined as long-term debt and finance lease obligations, including the current portion, minus cash and cash equivalents. We believe that net debt is a useful measure because it helps to give investors a clear understanding of our financial position and is also used to calculate certain leverage ratios. (9) Ratio of net debt to adjusted EBITDA is defined as net

debt divided by adjusted EBITDA. We believe that ratio of net debt to adjusted EBITDA is a useful measure because it monitors the sustainability of our debt levels and our ability to take on additional debt against adjusted EBITDA, which is used as an operating performance measure.

Although these non-GAAP measures are frequently used by investors and securities analysts in their evaluations of companies in industries similar to ours, these non-GAAP measures have limitations as analytical tools, are not measurements of our performance under GAAP and should not be considered as alternatives to operating profit, net income or any other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP and should not be used by investors or other users of our financial statements in isolation for formulating decisions, as such non-GAAP measures exclude a number of important cash and non-cash charges.

You should be aware that our presentation of these and other non-GAAP financial measures in this press release may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies. A reconciliation of each of these non-GAAP measures to its most directly comparable financial measure calculated in accordance with GAAP is provided in this release.
