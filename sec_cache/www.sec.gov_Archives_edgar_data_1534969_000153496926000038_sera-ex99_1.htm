SERA PROGNOSTICS REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

Company Drives Significant Increases in Test Volume and Ordering Providers with Florida Becoming Second Medicaid State Covering Test

•
PreTRM® test volume increases 54% sequentially and approximately five-fold from 1Q26
•
Ordering healthcare providers increase 34% sequentially and approximately three-fold from 1Q26
•
Florida provides Medicaid coverage for PreTRM® with established fee schedule pricing
•
PreTRM® mentioned in European Association for Perinatal Medicine guidelines
•
Started CE Mark submission for PreTRM® Global in Europe
•
Company scales commercial organization to support anticipated test volume ramp in fiscal year 2027

Salt Lake City – November 4, 2026 – Sera Prognostics Inc., The Pregnancy Company® (Nasdaq: SERA), focused on improving maternal and neonatal health by providing innovative pregnancy biomarker information to doctors and patients, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“The third quarter marked an important step forward as we continued our transition from evidence generation to commercial execution,” said Zhenya Lindgardt, President and Chief Executive Officer of Sera Prognostics. “We delivered strong growth in physician adoption and test volume, saw expanded reimbursement through a second state Medicaid coverage win in Florida, advanced our commercial payer initiatives, and continued to strengthen our clinical and health economic evidence base. Furthermore, following strong supporting evidence, we are working with our notified body to submit our CE Mark for our kit-based PreTRM® Global test. We believe these accomplishments further validate our strategy and position us well as we work toward establishing PreTRM as a foundational component of prenatal care.”

Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:

•
PreTRM test volumes increased 54% sequentially and almost five-fold from the 1Q26 following the PRIME study publication early this year and recent commercial leadership team hires.
•
Total PreTRM test revenue in the quarter was $42,000 representing a 163% increase on a year-over-year basis. Revenue was negatively impacted in the quarter due to lower test average selling prices (ASPs) due to a higher mix of low or non-reimbursed tests.
•
Operating expenses in the quarter declined 11% sequentially from the 2Q26 followed cost reduction initiatives that were implemented in June 2026. This was partially offset by increased commercial spending to support commercial launches in Illinois and Florida following the establishment of Medicaid coverage. Additionally, the company has approximately $0.4 million in the quarter in severance related expenses tied to the restructuring.
•
Cash, cash equivalents and marketable investment securities ended the quarter at $72.8 million with total cash burn from operations in the fiscal third quarter of $7.3 million.

 

 

Fiscal Third Quarter 2026 Business Highlights:

•
Following the passage of Florida’s biomarker testing legislation (Chapter 2024-249) in 2024, the Florida Agency for Health Care Administration has added the PreTRM test’s PLA code 0247U to its covered biomarker test list and to the Florida Medicaid laboratory fee schedule. This creates biomarker-testing coverage requirements within Medicaid managed care and establishes coverage for the test for Florida Medicaid patients. Florida has approximately 4 million Medicaid beneficiaries and approximately 90,000 Medicaid births per year. This brings total covered Medicaid lives to approximately 7 million patients.
•
After the enactment of Illinois legislation establishing Medicaid coverage for the PreTRM test, the test’s PLA code 0247U was added to the Illinois Medicaid fee schedule at $525, with no prior authorization required. We have completed Medicaid credentialing in the state of Illinois and are actively working on contracting with Medicaid managed care plans in the state.
•
Launched our fourth payer partnership program, a state-based initiative with a national payer designed to expand implementation of PreTRM test-guided care and further demonstrate the clinical and economic value of the PreTRM test within real-world payer populations. We are currently in discussions with one of the largest commercial insurers regarding a large multi-state, multi-thousand-patient pilot with planned initiation in fiscal year 2027.
•
Our sales team expanded by 25% in the third quarter of 2026 to support Illinois commercialization and further expanded the team in early October 2026 to support our Florida commercialization plan.
•
As planned based upon European registration study results, we are now starting the submission process for CE Mark with our notified body.
•
The European Association of Perinatal Medicine referenced the PreTRM test as a potential tool to support improved prediction of preterm birth risk in its recently released guidelines.
•
Published a PRIME randomized controlled trial subgroup analysis in The Journal of Maternal-Fetal & Neonatal Medicine demonstrating that PreTRM test-guided care resulted in a 22% reduction in NICU admissions among first-time mothers, further strengthening the clinical evidence supporting the use of the PreTRM test to identify risk and inform proactive pregnancy management.
•
Two analyses from the PRIME randomized controlled trial were presented at the 2026 Society for Maternal-Fetal Medicine Global Congress, including an oral presentation evaluating outcomes among first-time mothers and a health economic analysis recognized among the top five posters in its session.

About Sera Prognostics, Inc.

Sera Prognostics is a leading health diagnostics company dedicated to improving the lives of women and babies through precision pregnancy care. Sera’s mission is to provide early, pivotal pregnancy information to improve the health of mothers and newborns, resulting in reductions in the costs of healthcare delivery. Sera has a robust pipeline of innovative diagnostic tests focused on the early prediction of preterm birth risk and other complications of pregnancy. Sera’s precision medicine PreTRM® Test reports to a physician the individualized risk of spontaneous premature delivery in a pregnancy, enabling earlier proactive interventions in women with higher risk. Sera Prognostics is headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah.

About Preterm Birth

Preterm birth is defined as any birth before 37 weeks’ gestation and is the leading cause of illness and death in newborns. The 2025 March of Dimes Report Card shows that, for the fourth consecutive year, the United States earned a D+ grade for preterm birth, marking the longest stretch of the lowest grade in Report Card history. Prematurity is associated with a significantly increased risk of major long-term medical complications, including learning disabilities, cerebral palsy, chronic respiratory illness, intellectual disability, seizures, and vision and hearing loss, and can generate significant costs throughout the lives of affected children. The annual health care costs to manage short- and long-term complications of prematurity in the United States were estimated to be approximately $25 billion for 2016.

About the PreTRM® Test

The PreTRM® Test is the only broadly validated, commercially available blood-based biomarker test that provides an early, accurate and individualized risk prediction for spontaneous preterm birth in asymptomatic singleton pregnancies. The PreTRM® Test measures and analyzes proteins in the blood that are highly predictive of preterm birth. The PreTRM® Test permits physicians to identify, during the weeks 18 to 21 of pregnancy, which women are at increased risk for preterm birth and its complications, enabling more informed, personalized clinical decisions based on each woman’s individual risk. The PreTRM® Test is ordered by a medical professional.
