ACCO BRANDS REPORTS THIRD QUARTER RESULTS

•
Reported net sales of $380 million, in line with outlook
•
Diluted EPS of $0.12 and Adjusted diluted EPS of $0.28, both include $0.11 per share in net tariff refunds
•
Closed on the acquisition of Trust on October 1, 2026
•
Raises full-year 2026 outlook to reflect tariff refunds and the acquisition

 

 

LAKE ZURICH, ILLINOIS, October 29, 2026 - ACCO Brands Corporation (NYSE: ACCO) today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

"We delivered third quarter sales consistent with our outlook, driven by a solid back-to-school season in both North America and Mexico and stabilizing trends in Workspace Solutions. Adjusted EPS included net tariff refunds, with underlying operations in line with outlook. We generated strong cashflows and continue to pay down debt, ending September with a consolidated leverage ratio of 3.6x," stated ACCO Brands' President and Chief Executive Officer, Tom Tedford.

 

"Technology peripherals remain our strongest long-term growth opportunity, and we closed on the acquisition of Trust, which will expand our tech peripheral portfolio to approach $500 million of pro forma annual sales. With Kensington, PowerA, EPOS and Trust, we now have a scaled platform across consumer and enterprise channels in large growth categories. Our focus is integrating these brands and delivering synergy opportunities," added Mr. Tedford.

 

Third Quarter Tariff Refund Impact

 

During the third quarter of 2026, net tariff refunds benefited consolidated operating income by $15.1 million. This resulted in a net per share benefit of $0.11 to our diluted earnings per

1

 

share and 400 basis points of improvement in the consolidated adjusted operating margin in the quarter.

 

Third Quarter Results

 

Third quarter net sales decreased 0.9 percent to $380.3 million from $383.7 million in 2025. This decrease reflects 4.9 percent growth from the EPOS acquisition and 0.4 percent from favorable foreign exchange, which were more than offset by a 6.2 percent decline in comparable sales. Technology peripherals experienced softness due to global industry-wide challenges. Our overall back-to-school season was positive, with more sales being recognized in the second quarter.

 

Operating income was $30.3 million, compared with $26.0 million in 2025. Current-year operating income included one-time charges of $5.9 million, compared with $1.5 million in the prior year. Adjusted operating income increased to $47.8 million, up from $39.2 million in 2025, reflecting cost savings, partly offset by lower mix. Current-year operating income and adjusted operating income include $15.1 million in net tariff refunds.

 

Net income was $11.5 million, or $0.12 per share, compared with $4.0 million, or $0.04 per share, in 2025. The improvement in net income reflects items noted above in operating income and adjusted operating income, plus differences in discrete tax items. Adjusted net income increased to $27.2 million, from $19.5 million in 2025, and adjusted earnings per share rose to $0.28 from $0.21 in 2025.

 

Business Segment Results

 

ACCO Brands Americas – Third quarter segment net sales of $219.8 million decreased 3.4 percent from $227.6 million in the prior year. Growth from the EPOS acquisition of 2.0 percent and favorable foreign currency of 1.0 percent was more than offset by a 6.4 percent comparable sales decline. Lower demand for technology peripherals was offset by growth in Latin America.

 

Third quarter operating income was $25.4 million, compared with $24.7 million a year earlier, with larger non-recurring items this year. Adjusted operating income increased to $38.1 million, from $32.7 million in the prior year. The increase in both operating and adjusted operating income reflects net tariff refunds, partly offset by lower mix and volume.

 

ACCO Brands International – Third quarter segment net sales were $160.5 million, an increase of 2.8 percent from $156.1 million in the prior year. The EPOS acquisition increased sales by 9.2 percent and unfavorable foreign exchange reduced sales 0.4 percent.

2

 

Comparable sales were down 6.0 percent year-over-year, reflecting lower demand for technology peripherals and softness in Australia.

 

Third quarter operating income was $12.4 million, compared with operating income of $10.5 million in the prior year. Adjusted operating income was $17.0 million, compared with $15.9 million in the prior year. The increase in both reflects pricing and cost savings, partly offset by lower mix.

 

Nine Month Results

 

Net sales of $1,139.1 million increased 3.9 percent from $1,095.9 million in 2025. The EPOS acquisition increased sales by $56.4 million, or 5.1 percent, and favorable foreign exchange increased sales by $27.6 million, or 2.5 percent. Comparable sales decreased 3.7 percent due to industry-wide trends in technology peripherals, partially mitigated by stronger demand for learning and creative categories in the Americas segment. Globally, workspace solutions trends have moderated versus the prior year.

 

Operating income was $50.2 million compared with $52.3 million in 2025. Current-year operating income included restructuring and other non-recurring items totaling $23.2 million, compared with $13.2 million in the prior year. Prior-year operating income benefited from a gain on sale of assets of $6.9 million. Adjusted operating income increased to $107.6 million from $93.2 million in 2025. Current-year operating income and adjusted operating income include $15.1 million of net tariff refunds.

 

Net income was $45.0 million, or $0.47 per share, compared with a net income of $20.0 million, or $0.21 per share, in 2025. Nine-month net income benefited from a $33.8 million bargain purchase gain related to the preliminary purchase price allocation, partially offset by costs for the EPOS acquisition, and reflects items noted above in operating income. Prior-year net income was positively impacted by discrete tax items, notably the Company's settlelment of the outstanding tax assessments in Brazil. Adjusted net income was $56.4 million compared with $43.3 million in 2025, and adjusted earnings per share were $0.59 per share compared with $0.46 per share in 2025, reflecting items noted above in operating income and adjusted operating income.

 

Cash Flow, Debt and Dividend

 

Year to date, free cash flow was $89.0 million including $21.3 million of tariff refunds, compared with $25.8 million in the prior year. The cash balance of $174.7 million at quarter

3

 

end included cash held for the acquisition of Trust, which closed on October 1. The Company's consolidated leverage ratio was 3.6x as of September 30, 2026.

 

Year to date, the Company has paid dividends of $20.8 million.

 

On October 23, 2026, ACCO Brands announced that its board of directors declared a regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.075 per share, payable on December 9, 2026 to stockholders of record at the close of business on November 20, 2026.

 

Full Year 2026 Outlook

 

For the full year, the Company expects reported sales to increase 4.0 percent to 6.0 percent, updated to include one quarter of expected sales from the acquired Trust business. Full-year adjusted EPS is expected to be $0.98 to $1.02 inclusive of $0.12 from the tariff refund. The Company expects 2026 free cash flow of approximately $100 million and the consolidated leverage ratio of approximately 3.5x at year-end.

We develop, nurture and acquire a portfolio of distinct, innovative and widely recognized brands, including EPOS®, Five Star®, Kensington®, Leitz®, Mead®, PowerA®, Tilibra®, Trust® and many others. About Non-GAAP Financial Measures

We explain below how we calculate each of our non-GAAP financial measures. This is followed by a reconciliation of our current period and historical non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures.

 

We use our non-GAAP financial measures both to explain our results to stockholders and the investment community and in the internal evaluation and management of our business. We believe our non-GAAP financial measures provide management and investors with a more complete understanding of our underlying operational results and trends, facilitate meaningful period-to-period comparisons and enhance an overall understanding of our past and future financial performance.

 

Our non-GAAP financial measures exclude certain items that may have a material impact upon our reported financial results such as restructuring charges, the impact of foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations, unusual tax items, goodwill and indefinite-lived trade name impairments and charges, and other non-recurring items that we consider to be outside of our core operations. Tariff costs and related refunds are part of our underlying operational results and are not included in the company's non-GAAP measures. On an interim basis, we also calculate adjusted income tax expense using our estimated annual income tax rate. These measures should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for, or superior to, the directly comparable GAAP financial measures and should be read in connection with the Company’s financial statements presented in accordance with GAAP.

 

Our non-GAAP financial measures include the following:

 

Comparable Sales: Represents net sales excluding the impact of acquisitions, if any, with current-period foreign operation sales translated at prior-year currency rates. We believe comparable sales are useful to investors and management because they reflect underlying sales and sales trends without the effect of acquisitions and fluctuations in foreign exchange rates and facilitate meaningful period-to-period comparisons. We sometimes refer to comparable sales as comparable net sales.

 

Adjusted Gross Profit, Adjusted Operating Income (Loss)/Adjusted Income (Loss) Before Taxes/Adjusted Net Income (Loss)/Adjusted Net Income (Loss) Per Diluted Share: Represents gross profit, operating income (loss), income (loss) before taxes, net income (loss), and net income (loss) per diluted share excluding restructuring and goodwill and indefinite-lived trade name impairment charges, the amortization of intangibles, bargain purchase gain, non-recurring items, other income/expense, adjustments to reflect the estimated annual tax rate and discrete income tax adjustments, including income tax related to the foregoing. We believe these adjusted non-GAAP financial measures are useful to investors and management because they reflect our underlying operating performance before items that we consider to be outside our core operations and facilitate meaningful period-to-period comparisons. Senior management’s incentive compensation is derived, in part, using adjusted operating income and adjusted net income per diluted share, which is derived from adjusted net income. We sometimes refer to adjusted net income per diluted share as adjusted earnings per share or adjusted EPS.

 

Adjusted Income Tax Expense (Benefit): Represents income tax expense (benefit) excluding the tax effect of the items that have been excluded from adjusted income (loss) before taxes, unusual income tax items such as the impact of tax audits and changes in laws, and other discrete tax items. We believe our adjusted income tax expense (benefit) is useful to investors because it reflects our income tax calculated using the estimated annual tax rate before discrete tax items that we consider to be outside our core operations and facilitates meaningful period-to-period comparisons. For interim periods, the income tax expense (benefit) is calculated using the estimated annual income tax rate.

 

Adjusted EBITDA: Represents net income excluding the effects of depreciation, stock-based compensation expense, amortization of intangibles, interest expense, net, other (income) expense, net, and income tax expense, restructuring and goodwill and indefinite-lived trade name impairment charges, bargain purchase gain, and other non-recurring items. We believe adjusted EBITDA is useful to investors because it reflects our underlying cash profitability and adjusts for certain non-cash charges and other items that we consider to be outside our core operations and facilitates meaningful period-to-period comparisons. In addition, this calculation of adjusted EBITDA is used in our loan agreement to calculate our leverage ratio covenant.

 

Free Cash Flow: Free cash flow represents cash flow from operating activities less cash used for additions to property, plant and equipment. We believe free cash flow is useful to investors because they measure our available cash flow for paying dividends, reducing debt, repurchasing shares and funding acquisitions.

 

Net Debt: Represents balance sheet debt plus unamortized debt origination costs and less any cash and cash equivalents.

 

Consolidated Leverage Ratio: Represents net debt divided by trailing twelve months adjusted EBITDA.

 

We also provide forward-looking non-GAAP comparable sales, adjusted earnings per share, free cash flow, adjusted EBITDA and historical and forward-looking consolidated leverage ratio. We do not provide a reconciliation of these forward-looking and historical non-GAAP measures to GAAP because the GAAP financial measure is not currently available and management cannot reliably predict all the necessary components of such non-GAAP measures without unreasonable effort or expense due to the inherent difficulty of forecasting and quantifying certain amounts that are necessary for such a reconciliation, including adjustments that could be made for restructuring, integration and acquisition-related expenses, the variability of our tax rate and the impact of foreign currency fluctuation and acquisitions, and other charges reflected in our historical results. The probable significance of each of these items is high and, based on historical experience, could be material.
