Virtuix Expects to Report Strong Second Quarter Revenue Growth Following Meta Quest Launch; Schedules Q2 FY2027 Earnings Call

 

Company to Host Second Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Results Conference Call on Monday, November 16, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time

 

AUSTIN, TEXAS – October 27, 2026 – Virtuix Holdings Inc. (NASDAQ: VTIX) (“Virtuix” or the “Company”), a leading developer of AI-driven, full-body simulation systems, today announced that it expects to report strong revenue growth for the second quarter of fiscal year 2027, ended September 30, 2026, driven in part by increased Omni One sales following the Company’s June launch of Omni One for Quest in collaboration with Meta. These expectations are preliminary and remain subject to completion of the Company’s quarter-end financial closing procedures. Virtuix will host a conference call on Monday, November 16, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time to discuss its financial results and provide an update on commercial developments across consumer entertainment, defense simulation, and healthcare.

 

The call will also cover Virtuix’s expanding defense and enterprise footprint, including recent sales to the U.S. Army, U.S. Navy, and U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, developments in humanoid robotics and enterprise applications, and the Company’s upcoming autism therapy pilot.

 

Jan Goetgeluk, Virtuix’s Chief Executive Officer and Chairman, and Thomas McGinnis, Chief Financial Officer, will host the conference call and present a detailed overview of the Company’s fiscal second quarter performance, commercial traction, outlook for fiscal year 2027, and key growth initiatives designed to support long-term shareholder value creation. The presentation will be followed by a question-and-answer period.

 

“We are excited to share what we anticipate will be a standout quarter for Virtuix, driven by meaningful growth in Omni One revenues following our successful launch of Omni One for Quest in collaboration with Meta,” said Jan Goetgeluk, Chairman and CEO of Virtuix. “Combined with accelerating momentum in our defense business, we believe these developments reflect the strength of our execution and the significant growth opportunities ahead.”

About Virtuix

 

Virtuix Holdings Inc. (NASDAQ: VTIX) is a developer of AI-driven, full-body immersive simulation systems and the creator of the “Omni” omni-directional treadmill. Virtuix’s technology lets users physically move through virtual and AI-generated environments. The Company’s products are deployed across consumer entertainment, defense training and simulation, robotics, healthcare, research, and enterprise applications involving organizations including Meta, NASA, Tesla, KBR, and Sirica Therapeutics. The Company’s growing federal and defense footprint includes programs, deployments, and research initiatives involving the U.S. Army, Marine Corps, Navy, and Air Force.

 

Virtuix continues to expand the Omni brand from immersive entertainment into a broader, full-body simulation technology platform serving multiple commercial and government markets. For more information, visit virtuix.com or the Company’s new Investor Relations website at invest.virtuix.com.
