BioStem Technologies Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results; Grows Hospital Revenue for Second Consecutive Quarter as Customers Expand Use of Portfolio

 

Hospital revenue grew sequentially to $6.8 million; customers purchasing two or more product lines up 14% from the second quarter

In-house production of Neox® and Clarix® on track to begin in the first half of 2027

Raises full-year 2026 revenue guidance

 

POMPANO BEACH, FL, November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — BioStem Technologies, Inc. (Nasdaq: BSEM) (“BioStem” or the “Company”), a leading regenerative medicine company focused on the development, manufacturing, and commercialization of perinatal tissue allograft products, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and raised full year 2026 revenue guidance.

 

“We grew hospital revenue again this quarter, and importantly, are doing so by adding new accounts and increasing penetration with existing customers. The number of customers buying two or more of our product lines grew 14% from the second quarter. We also completed the final step of our commercial infrastructure build-out, and with in-house production of Neox and Clarix expected to begin in the first half of 2027, we are on track to become a fully vertically integrated company with those product lines,” said Jason Matuszewski, Chair and CEO of BioStem. “With these foundations in place, we are focused on strengthening the balance sheet, raising our gross margin, reducing operating expenses and narrowing our adjusted EBITDA loss as we work toward breakeven.”

 

Recent Business Highlights

•
Generated net revenue of $7.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, with hospital revenue increasing sequentially to $6.8 million
•
Deepened adoption within existing accounts, customers purchasing two or more of the Company’s product lines increased 14% from the second quarter, customers purchasing three or more lines increased more than 40%
•
Grew the number of customers ordering in consecutive quarters by 6% from the second quarter
•
Completed the integration of sales logistics, operational support, invoicing and collections from BioTissue effective at the beginning of August, with the Company's CRM platform now live and integrated with its ERP system
•
Added the VENDAJE product family to the Company's two largest existing GPO agreements in September
•
Technology transfer of Neox and Clarix manufacturing to the Company’s Pompano Beach facility on track, with in-house production expected to begin in the first half of 2027
•
Top-line results from the Company’s VLU study scheduled to be reported at the Innovations in Wound Healing meeting in early December

 

 

 

Recent Corporate Highlights

•
Closed a private placement with multiple institutional investors on September 30, 2026, generating gross proceeds of approximately $3 million, with the potential for up to approximately $5.6 million in additional gross proceeds from the exercise of accompanying warrants with an exercise price of $3.83 per share
•
Established a $40 million committed equity facility with Roth Principal Investments, LLC; sale of shares under the facility may not begin until early April 2027 unless the applicable restriction is waived
•
Working with BioTissue to restructure the terms of the $10.0 million Clearance Payment in order to lower near-term cash obligations
•
Finalizing a cost reduction plan expected to reduce annualized cash operating expenses by approximately $10 to $12 million compared with the current run rate, with the full benefit beginning in the first quarter of 2027

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Net revenue was $7.6 million, compared to $7.9 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $10.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. Hospital revenue was $6.8 million compared to $6.7 million in the second quarter of 2026, and physician office revenue was $0.8 million compared to $1.1 million in the second quarter of 2026.

 

Gross profit was $4.3 million, representing a gross margin of 56%, compared to $4.8 million and 61% in the second quarter of 2026, and $9.3 million and 88.5% in the prior year period. The sequential decrease in gross margin was in line with expectations and primarily reflected higher pricing under the BioTissue supply agreement following the expiration of the initial discounted pricing.

 

Operating expenses totaled $14.1 million, compared to $13.2 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $7.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The sequential increase was driven primarily by higher general and administrative expenses related to uplisting and financing activity and payroll taxes on RSU vesting, partly offset by lower research and development spend.

 

GAAP net loss was ($11.4) million, or ($0.64) per share, compared to ($9.0)million, or ($0.52) per share, in the second quarter of 2026.

 

Adjusted EBITDA loss was ($6.4) million, compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $2.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. A reconciliation of GAAP net (loss) income to Adjusted EBITDA loss is included at the end of this press release.

As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents totaled $1.0 million, compared to $7.0 million as of June 30, 2026. Cash used in operations in the third quarter was $8.8 million, compared to $5.5 million in the second quarter. During the quarter, the company closed a private placement on September 30, 2026 with gross proceeds of approximately $3 million, with the potential for up to approximately $5.6 million in additional gross proceeds from the exercise of accompanying warrants with an exercise price of $3.83 per

 

share. Subsequent to the quarter, the Company’s CEO and COO exercised options generating approximately $1.0 million in gross proceeds. No shares have been sold under the Company’s committed equity facility, and sales under the facility are restricted until early April 2027 unless waived.

 

Financial Outlook

BioStem now expects its revenue for full year 2026 to be in the range of $28 million to $29 million, an increase from the prior outlook of $26 million to $29 million.

About BioStem Technologies, Inc. (Nasdaq: BSEM): BioStem Technologies, Inc. is a publicly traded, biomedical innovator, focused on developing, manufacturing and commercializing advanced allograft solutions derived from perinatal tissue. The company leverages its industry-leading proprietary BioRetain®, CryoTek® and SteriTek® processing technologies, designed to optimize the preservation of the natural properties of these tissues, supporting their use in clinical settings. Its allografts are used by clinicians across a wide range of specialties. With a growing portfolio of products, expanding clinical research initiatives, and a national commercial footprint, BioStem is committed to advancing innovation in regenerative medicine.

 

BioStem Technologies’ quality management system and standard operating procedures have been reviewed and accredited by the Association for Advancing Tissue and Biologics (“AATB”). These systems and procedures are established in compliance with current Good Tissue Practices (“cGTP”) and current Good Manufacturing Practices (“cGMP”). BioStem’s portfolio of quality brands includes its Neox®, Clarix®, VENDAJE® and American Amnion™ product lines.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures:

Our management uses financial measures that are not in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States, or GAAP, in addition to financial measures in accordance with GAAP to evaluate our operating results. These non-GAAP financial measures should be considered supplemental to, and not a substitute for, our reported financial results prepared in accordance with GAAP. Our management uses EBITDA, which we calculate as net income (loss) adjusted for interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, and Adjusted EBITDA, which we calculate as EBITDA further adjusted for share-based compensation expense, SEC registration and related transaction costs, consideration payable extension costs, PIPE and warrant issuance costs and other non-recurring costs, to evaluate our operating performance and trends and make planning decisions. Our management believes EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA help identify underlying trends in our business that could otherwise be masked by the effect of the items that we exclude. Accordingly, we believe that EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA provide useful information to investors and others in understanding and evaluating our operating results, enhancing the overall understanding of our past performance and future prospects, and allowing for greater transparency with respect to key financial metrics used by our management in its financial and operational decision-making.

 

Cash operating expenses is a non-GAAP financial measure that we calculate as GAAP operating expenses excluding share-based compensation, depreciation and amortization, and other non-cash items. We are unable to provide a reconciliation of the expected reduction in annualized cash operating expenses, or of our targeted Adjusted EBITDA, to the most directly comparable GAAP measures without unreasonable efforts. This is because we cannot reliably predict the amount or timing of certain items, including share-based compensation, changes in the fair value of our warrant liability, transaction related costs and other non-recurring items. These items are uncertain, depend on factors outside our control, and could have a significant impact on our GAAP results.
