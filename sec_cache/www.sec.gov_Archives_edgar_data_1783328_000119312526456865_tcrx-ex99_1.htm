TScan Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Corporate Update

 

Announced strategic reorganization to focus on in vivo-engineered TCR-T cell therapy for solid tumors

 

Advancing PRAME- and MAGE-A4-targeted in vivo TCR-T candidates for solid tumors through IND-enabling studies; on track to dose first patient in Q4 2027

 

Paused further enrollment in Phase 3 ALLOHA-2™ study of TSC-101; allowing data to mature and actively seeking strategic partners

 

Cash and cash equivalents fund operations into the fourth quarter of 2027

 

WALTHAM, Mass., November 12, 2026 -- TScan Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: TCRX), a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on the development of T cell receptor (TCR)-engineered T cell (TCR-T) therapies for the treatment of patients with cancer, today reported financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

 

“This quarter marked an important step in accelerating TScan’s solid tumor program. We are concentrating all our efforts on our in vivo-engineered TCR-T cell approach for solid tumor indications, where we see an opportunity to bring an exciting new treatment modality to patients in desperate need of better treatment options. Our team is working to advance our two lead candidates through preclinical development and complete the studies required to support clinical entry, with the goal of dosing our first patient by the fourth quarter of next year,” said Gavin MacBeath, Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer. “At the same time, we are actively pursuing strategic partnerships to advance our heme malignancies and autoimmunity programs. We are excited about the progress we have made this year on our in vivo TCR-T candidates and look forward to sharing more of our preclinical data and FDA feedback in early 2027.”

 

Recent Corporate Highlights

 

•
In September, the Company announced a strategic reorganization to prioritize its in vivo solid tumor program. TScan’s first two therapeutic candidates, one targeting PRAME and the other targeting MAGE-A4, were advanced to IND-enabling studies.
•
The Company also provided updated data in September from Cohort C of its Phase 1 ALLOHA™ study of TSC-101 in heme malignancies. At the time of the update, all 14 patients in Cohort C of the study showed complete donor chimerism at their last assessment, including the two patients who had previously relapsed. The Company paused further enrollment in the Phase 3 ALLOHA-2™ study of TSC-101 due to insufficient capital needed to complete the trial and is actively seeking strategic partnerships. TScan remains committed to tracking the patients enrolled in Cohort C of the Phase 1 study, as well as the 7 patients who were enrolled on the treatment arm of the Phase 3 ALLOHA-2™ study and the five patients enrolled on the control arm of ALLOHA-2.

 

Pipeline Progress and Upcoming Anticipated Milestones

 

Solid Tumor Program: The Company’s strategy is to treat patients with in vivo-engineered TCR-T therapy candidates, initially as singleplexed therapy and ultimately as multiplexed therapy. The Company currently has two candidates in IND-enabling studies: one targeting PRAME and the other targeting MAGE-A4.

 

 

Anticipated Milestones

•
Disclose preclinical data and FDA feedback from pre-IND meetings on TSC-303-A02 (PRAME) and TSC-302-A02 (MAGE-A4) in Q1 2027.
•
File the first IND for in vivo-engineered TCR-T for solid tumors in Q3 2027, with first patient dosed in Q4 2027.
•
Report initial Phase 1 data on in vivo-engineered TCR-T in H1 2028.

 

Heme Malignancies Program: TScan’s TCR-T therapy candidate, TSC-101, is designed to treat residual disease and prevent relapse in patients with heme malignancies undergoing allogeneic hematopoietic cell transplantation (HCT). TScan is currently seeking strategic partners for this program.

 

Anticipated Milestones

•
Share updated data on patients treated in Cohort C of the Phase 1 ALLOHA™ study at the upcoming 68th American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting and Exposition in Q4 2026.

Title: Phase 1 (ALLOHA) trial cohort C: TSC-101 eliminates recipient hematopoietic cells and demonstrates potential for improved outcomes in patients with AML, ALL, or MDS post-allogeneic RIC HCT

Publication Number: 4178

Presentation Date and Time: December 13, 2026, 6:00 - 8:00 pm E.T.

•
Share updated data, including >1 year of follow-up time, on Cohort C patients of the ALLOHA™ study, and new data, including >6 months of follow-up time, on patients enrolled in the ALLOHA-2™ study, in the first half of 2027.

 

Autoimmunity Program: The Company has discovered novel targets for ankylosing spondylitis and other HLA-B*27-associated autoimmune disorders and is currently seeking strategic partners for this program.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Revenue: Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.6 million, compared to $2.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily due to timing of research activities pursuant to the Company’s collaboration agreement with Amgen, which was terminated in August 2026.

 

R&D Expenses: Research and development (R&D) expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $25.3 million, compared to $31.7 million for the third quarter of 2025. The decrease of $6.4 million was primarily driven by a decrease in laboratory supplies, research materials, and studies due to the timing in the purchase of supplies and consumables, and decrease spend on contracted services, as well as savings in connection with the 2025 workforce reduction. R&D expenses included non-cash stock compensation expense of $1.0 million and $1.7 million for the third quarter of 2026 and 2025, respectively.

 

G&A Expenses: General and administrative (G&A) expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $8.3 million, compared to $7.9 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase of $0.5 million was primarily due to costs incurred in connection with the 2026 reorganization. G&A expenses included non-cash stock compensation expense of $1.1 million and $1.3 million for the third quarter of 2026 and 2025, respectively.

 

Net Loss: Net loss was $33.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $35.7 million for the third quarter of 2025, and included net interest expense of $0.1 million and net interest income of $1.3 million, respectively.

 

Cash Position: Cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026, were $73.0 million, excluding $5.0 million of restricted cash. The Company believes that its existing cash resources will be sufficient to fund its current operating plan into the fourth quarter of 2027. The Company did not achieve certain non-covenant related milestones by June 30, 2026 as provided under its existing debt agreement. The updated cash runway therefore reflects commencement of the two-year term loan amortization beginning in the fourth quarter of 2026.

 

 

Share Count: As of September 30, 2026, the Company had 67,779,255 issued and outstanding shares of common stock, consisting of 63,502,667 shares of voting common stock and 4,276,588 shares of non-voting common stock, as well as 62,246,707 outstanding pre-funded warrants to purchase shares of voting common stock at an exercise price of $0.0001 per share. Pro forma outstanding shares, inclusive of both common stock and pre-funded warrants, were 130,025,962 as of September 30, 2026.

 

About TScan Therapeutics, Inc.

 

TScan is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on the development of T cell receptor (TCR)-engineered T cell (TCR-T) therapies for the treatment of patients with cancer. The Company is currently advancing its two lead therapeutic candidates through IND-enabling studies to treat solid tumors using in vivo-engineered TCR-T cells and is advancing a strategy to treat patients with multiplexed TCR-T to overcome the challenge of tumor heterogeneity. In addition, the Company is actively seeking partnerships for its heme and autoimmune programs. To learn more, visit www.tscan.com and connect with us on LinkedIn and X.
