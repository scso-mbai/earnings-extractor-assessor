Sirius XM Holdings Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Raises Full-Year Financial Guidance
 
•Third Quarter Consolidated Revenue was down slightly year-over-year to $2.15 billion
•Net Income increased 7% to $317 million from the prior year period
•Adjusted EBITDA grew 3% to $694 million1
•Free Cash Flow advanced 14% year-over-year to $293 million1
•Self-pay net additions of 5,000 improved 45,000 from the prior year, supported by lower self-pay churn of 1.5%
•SiriusXM raises full year 2026 guidance by $25 million for Revenue, Adjusted EBITDA and Free Cash Flow2


NEW YORK – October 29, 2026 – Sirius XM Holdings announced third quarter 2026 operating and financial results today, reporting revenue of $2.15 billion, down slightly year-over-year, primarily reflecting lower equipment revenue due to higher memory costs. Net income increased 7% to $317 million, while earnings per diluted common share declined 7% to $0.78, from $0.84 in the prior year period, reflecting the accounting treatment of convertible notes, including adjustments for fair value gains and assumed share dilution.

Adjusted EBITDA grew 3% year-over-year to $694 million in the third quarter, reflecting stable revenue and disciplined expense management, including lower programming expenses, greater automation in customer care and reduced sales and marketing spending. Adjusted EBITDA margin expanded one percentage point year-over-year to 32%. Free cash flow increased 14% year-over-year to $293 million, due to higher adjusted EBITDA, favorable working capital, lower interest expense and reduced capital expenditures.

“Our third-quarter performance reflects the progress we're making in building a stronger, more durable SiriusXM,” said Jennifer Witz, Chief Executive Officer. “We're stabilizing our subscriber base, deepening engagement through differentiated content and experiences, and expanding the reach and capabilities of our advertising platform. By consistently enhancing the value of a SiriusXM subscription, we're strengthening customer loyalty and creating more lasting relationships with listeners. At the same time, partnerships like YouTube Audio are expanding our premium advertising inventory and creating new opportunities to connect advertisers with audiences at scale. This momentum gives us the confidence to raise our full-year financial guidance and reinforces our conviction in SiriusXM's long-term value creation.”

“Our financial results reflect the increasing efficiency and cash-generating capacity of our business,” said Zac Coughlin, Chief Financial Officer. “Disciplined cost management and structural operating improvements drove Adjusted EBITDA growth, margin expansion and stronger profitability in the third quarter. We delivered approximately $1.1 billion in free cash flow for the first nine months of the year, achieved our long-term leverage target and strengthened our financial flexibility. Looking ahead, our more efficient operating model and declining satellite capital expenditures position us to meaningfully accelerate share repurchases in 2027 while continuing to invest in the business and deliver long-term value to shareholders.”

BUSINESS AND PROGRAMMING HIGHLIGHTS
SiriusXM continues to advance its strategy of being a home for fandom, deepening subscriber engagement and loyalty through differentiated content, exclusive experiences and enhanced discovery. Third-quarter average listening time increased approximately 5% year-over-year, with growth across all subscriber tenure groups, reflecting both new content discovery and deeper engagement with familiar favorites. These trends reinforce the value of SiriusXM's differentiated programming in strengthening listening habits and long-term subscriber loyalty. The company's 360L experience and streaming app continue to support engagement, with year-to-date SiriusXM app trends on pace to achieve the highest average listening time and most active days per listener since the new app launched in late 2023.
SiriusXM's exclusive programming and unparalleled talent relationships are at the heart of its value proposition, creating deeper fan connections and strengthening subscriber loyalty. The Dolly Parton Tribute Channel became the company's most-listened-to artist pop-up channel of 2026, while intimate performances with Shania Twain and John Mayer and special programming featuring Billy Joel and Talking Heads delivered unique fan experiences. Life with John Mayer also became SiriusXM's number one streamed artist channel.

Sports continues to drive engagement and subscription value. Listening to WWE Radio has grown nearly 20% since its July launch and rebrand, while monthly active sports listeners increased 4% year-over-year. SiriusXM also expanded its sports offering with 24 local sports channels, with approximately 22% of its 360L sports audience already tuning in and more than 80% listening from outside their home markets. Collectively, these channels would rank as SiriusXM's third-highest sports destination by number of listeners. In September, the company launched Sports Pass, a more affordable subscription combining live play-by-play, national coverage and local programming across all four major professional leagues, further broadening SiriusXM's addressable market.

SiriusXM is also expanding the reach and commercial value of its premium content. Howard Stern's expansion to HBO Max introduces his programming to new audiences while preserving the exclusive live experience for SiriusXM subscribers. The company also added Giggly Squad, Serialously and The Bald and the Beautiful to its leading podcast portfolio. Giggly Squad's sold-out, multi-night Madison Square Garden run underscores the cultural influence of its creators and the opportunity to monetize premium content across audio, video and live experiences.

SiriusXM Media continues to expand its advertising reach and monetization capabilities. With YouTube Audio, its platform now reaches approximately 255 million unduplicated monthly listeners, up from 170 million a year ago and representing nearly 90% of Americans ages 13 and older. Early campaigns have increased advertisers' unique reach by as much as 91%, demonstrating the value of combining YouTube's scale with SiriusXM Media's advertising capabilities. At its recent Built With Audio event, the company also highlighted AdsWizz, which powers one in four audio advertisements across the global open ecosystem. Together, SiriusXM Media's expanding reach, premium content and advertising technology position the company to capture more advertiser spending across budgets of all sizes.

SEGMENT HIGHLIGHTS
Sirius XM Holdings Inc. operates two complementary audio entertainment businesses — one of which is referred to as “SiriusXM” and the second of which is referred to as “Pandora and Off-Platform.” Further information regarding these two segments will be contained in the company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The financial highlights below exclude the impact of share-based payment expense.
 
Third Quarter 2026 SiriusXM Segment Highlights
SiriusXM Total Subscribers of 33 Million
SiriusXM self-pay subscribers increased by approximately 5,000 in the third quarter of 2026, an improvement of 45,000 compared to the prior year period. Performance was driven by growing adoption of companion subscriptions, which added 107,000 incremental self-pay net additions in the quarter, as well as continued benefits from the company’s continuous service initiative and momentum in automotive dealer extended-duration plans. These gains more than offset lower conversion rates. Self-pay monthly churn improved to approximately 1.5%, reflecting the continued benefits of the continuous service initiative and lower vehicle-related and non-pay churn, partially offset by slightly higher voluntary churn. SiriusXM ended the quarter with approximately 33 million total subscribers. Paid promotional subscribers increased 21,000 year-over-year to 1.594 million subscribers, reflecting the timing and mix of automaker promotional programs, partially offset by lower vehicle sales and trial starts. SiriusXM Revenue of $1.60 billion
In the third quarter of 2026, SiriusXM generated total revenue of $1.60 billion, relatively flat year-over-year. Subscriber revenue increased modestly to $1.50 billion, with Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) remaining largely stable at $15.18, reflecting rate increases on certain self-pay plans and improved discount mix as we transition customers from deeply discounted promotional offers to more sustainable pricing within our modular content packages. These benefits were partially offset by the growing mix of value-add and more affordable subscription offerings designed to broaden access and strengthen long-term retention. Advertising revenue declined 5% to $37 million, reflecting softer demand across news programming and direct-to-consumer advertisers, partially offset by continued strength in sports. Equipment revenue decreased 16% year-over-year, primarily reflecting higher memory costs associated with our hardware modules.

SiriusXM Gross Profit of $963 Million and Gross Margin of 60%
Total cost of services at SiriusXM decreased 2% to $641 million in the third quarter of 2026, from $653 million in the prior-year period. As a result, gross profit increased 1% to $963 million during the quarter from $958 million in the third quarter of 2025 and gross margin expanded one percentage point to 60%.

Third Quarter 2026 Pandora and Off-Platform Segment Highlights
Pandora and Off-Platform Third Quarter Revenue of $549 Million
Pandora and Off-Platform revenue increased modestly year-over-year to $549 million in the third quarter. Advertising revenue grew 1% year-over-year to $419 million, driven by strength in podcasting, programmatic advertising and technology fees, partially offset by softer streaming music advertising, reflecting lower listening hours. Subscriber revenue declined 2% to $130 million, primarily due to lower subscriber volumes. Pandora Plus and Pandora Premium ended the quarter with approximately 5.5 million self-pay subscribers, while monthly active users totaled 38.8 million.

Pandora and Off-Platform Third Quarter Gross Profit of $173 Million and Gross Margin of 32%
Total cost of services declined 1% to $376 million in the third quarter of 2026 from the prior year period, primarily reflecting lower revenue share and royalty expense. Despite relatively flat revenue, gross profit increased 2% to $173 million and gross margin expanded one percentage point to 32%.

ADDITIONAL FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS
Subscriber acquisition costs (SAC) were unchanged year-over-year at $107 million. Programming costs declined 6% year-over-year to $136 million, benefiting from favorable contract renewals, while customer service and billing expenses decreased 6% to $108 million, reflecting increased automation and lower call volumes. Sales and marketing expenses declined 4% year-over-year to $169 million, driven by more targeted in-car subscriber acquisition spending. Product and technology costs increased 4% year-over-year to $56 million, primarily reflecting higher data center and personnel related expenses, partially offset by increased capitalization of compensation costs. General and administrative expenses declined 4% year-over-year to $110 million, reflecting continued cost discipline.

Free cash flow totaled $293 million in the third quarter of 2026, increasing 14% from $257 million in the prior year period, due to higher Adjusted EBITDA, favorable working capital, lower interest expense and reduced capital expenditures.

During the quarter, SiriusXM returned capital to shareholders through $91 million in dividends and $33 million in share repurchases. The company ended the third quarter with a net debt-to-adjusted EBITDA ratio of 3.3x, providing greater financial flexibility to support its disciplined capital allocation strategy.

RAISED 2026 FULL-YEAR FINANCIAL AND OPERATING GUIDANCE
SiriusXM raised its full-year 2026 financial guidance for the second consecutive quarter, increasing each of its three key financial metrics by $25 million, reflecting continued operating momentum, improved profitability and strong cash flow generation. Updated full-year 2026 guidance includes:
•Total revenue: Approximately $8.55 billion,
•Adjusted EBITDA: Approximately $2.65 billion
•Free cash flow: Approximately $1.40 billion.

SiriusXM is the leading audio entertainment company in North America, with a portfolio of audio businesses including its flagship subscription entertainment service SiriusXM; the ad-supported and premium music streaming services of Pandora; an expansive podcast network; and a complete ecosystem of advertising solutions through SiriusXM Media and AdsWizz. SiriusXM offers live, on-demand, and human-curated programming across music, talk, news, sports, and podcasts, and the company reaches approximately 255 million monthly listeners across its platforms. With deep automotive manufacturer relationships and unique spectrum assets, SiriusXM is built to reach listeners wherever they are. The company connects fans to the voices, creators, and moments they love - creating communities where listeners engage, participate, and belong. For more about SiriusXM, please go to: https://www.siriusxm.com.
