Enanta Pharmaceuticals Reports Financial Results for its Fiscal Fourth Quarter and Year-Ended September 30, 2026

 

•
Completed Enrollment in LOTUS, a Phase 2b Trial of Zelicapavir in Pediatric Patients with Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV); Topline Data Expected in 1Q 2027
•
Initiated Dosing in RESOLVE, a Phase 2b/3 Trial of Zelicapavir in Adult Patients at High-Risk of Severe RSV Outcomes; Topline Phase 2b Data Expected in 2027
•
Topline Data from Phase 1 Trial of EDP-978, an Oral, Once-Daily KIT Inhibitor, Now Expected in 1Q 2027
•
Investigational New Drug (IND) Application Filing for EDP-903, an Oral, Once-Daily STAT6 Inhibitor, Now Expected in 2Q 2027
•
Selected MRGPRX2 Development Candidate EPS-5336 for the Treatment of Mast Cell-Driven Diseases Including Migraine; IND Filing Targeted for 4Q 2027
•
Announced the Planned Retirement of Longstanding President and Chief Executive Officer, Jay R. Luly, Ph.D. by February 2027, and the Promotion of Tara L. Kieffer, Ph.D. to Chief Operating Officer
•
Cash and Marketable Securities Totaling $194.5 Million at September 30, 2026, as well as Continuing Retained Royalties, with Cash Runway Expected to Fund Operations into Fiscal 2029

 

WATERTOWN, Mass., November 16, 2026 – Enanta Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (NASDAQ:ENTA), a clinical-stage biotechnology company dedicated to creating small molecule drugs for viral infections and immunological diseases, today reported financial results for its fiscal fourth quarter and year-ended September 30, 2026.

 

“During fiscal 2026 we made significant progress across our virology and immunology portfolios. In RSV, we completed enrollment in LOTUS, a Phase 2b trial of zelicapavir in pediatric patients, with topline data expected in the first quarter of 2027. We also began dosing patients in RESOLVE, a Phase 2b/3 trial of zelicapavir in adults at high risk of severe disease and anticipate reporting data from the Phase 2b portion in 2027,” said Jay R. Luly, Ph.D., President and Chief Executive Officer of Enanta Pharmaceuticals. “With no approved antiviral therapies available to treat RSV, we believe zelicapavir is well-positioned to address a substantial unmet need across both high-risk adult and pediatric populations. As we move into late-stage development, we continue to strengthen our leadership in RSV treatment and remain urgently focused on delivering zelicapavir to patients in need.”

 

 

 

Dr. Luly continued, “In parallel, we are advancing a growing immunology portfolio focused on developing best-in-class oral medicines that have the potential to raise the standard of care across multiple immune-mediated diseases. We expect to complete dosing in the EDP-978 Phase 1 trial by year-end 2026 and now plan to report data in the first quarter of 2027. We also plan to file INDs for EDP-903, our STAT6 clinical candidate, and EPS-5336, our newly selected MRGPRX2 development candidate, in the second and fourth quarters of 2027, respectively. We remain confident in our ability to deliver compounds with best-in-disease potential and look forward to multiple catalysts in the first half of next year.”

 

Fiscal Fourth Quarter and Year-Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Results

Total revenue was $12.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, which consisted of royalty revenue derived from worldwide net sales of AbbVie’s hepatitis C virus (HCV) regimen MAVYRET®/MAVIRET® (glecaprevir/pibrentasvir), compared to $15.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. For the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, total revenue was $63.0 million compared to $65.3 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease in the quarter and year-over-year revenue is due to a decline in AbbVie’s sales of MAVYRET®/MAVIRET®.

 

A portion (54.5%) of Enanta’s ongoing royalty revenue from AbbVie’s net sales of MAVYRET®/MAVIRET® is paid to OMERS, one of Canada’s largest defined benefit pension plans, pursuant to a royalty sale transaction affecting royalties earned after June 2023. For financial reporting purposes, the transaction was treated as debt, with the upfront purchase payment of $200.0 million recorded as a liability. Each quarter, Enanta records 100% of the royalty earned as revenue and then amortizes the debt liability proportionally as 54.5% of the cash royalty payments are paid to OMERS through June 30, 2032, subject to a cap of 1.42 times the purchase price, after which point 100% of the cash royalty payments will be retained by Enanta. Interest expense was $3.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and $14.1 million for the twelve months ended September 30, 2026. This compares to interest expense of $2.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025 and $7.7 million for the twelve months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in the quarter and year-over-year interest expense was due to an increase in forecasted royalty revenue from AbbVie’s net sales of MAVYRET®/MAVIRET® during the first half of Enanta’s 2026 fiscal year.

 

Research and development expenses were $26.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $23.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in quarterly research and development expenses was due to an increase in clinical trial expenses for Enanta’s RSV programs compared to the prior year period, driven by the Company’s RESOLVE and LOTUS studies. For the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, research and development expenses were $88.5 million compared to $106.7 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease in the year-over-year research and development expenses was due to timing of clinical trial expenses for Enanta’s RSV programs, partially offset by increased costs associated with the Company’s immunology programs.

 

 

 

General and administrative expenses totaled $8.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $9.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. For the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, general and administrative expenses were $36.8 million compared to $43.9 million in 2025. The decrease in the quarter and year-over-year general and administrative expenses was due to a decrease in stock-based compensation expenses and a decrease in legal expenses related to the Company’s patent infringement lawsuit against Pfizer.

 

Interest and investment income, net, totaled $1.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $2.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. For the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, interest and investment income, net, totaled $8.2 million compared to $9.4 million in 2025. The decrease in the quarter and year-over-year interest and investment income was due to lower interest rates.

Enanta recorded an income tax benefit of less than $0.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to an income tax expense of less than $0.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. Enanta recorded an income tax expense of less than $0.1 million for the twelve months ended September 30, 2026 and an income tax benefit of $1.7 million for the twelve months ended September 30, 2025. Enanta recorded an income tax benefit in the twelve months ended September 30, 2025 due to interest earned on a federal income tax refund received during fiscal 2025.

 

Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $23.7 million, or a loss of $0.81 per diluted common share, compared to a net loss of $18.7 million, or a loss of $0.87 per diluted common share, for the corresponding period in 2025. For the twelve months ended September 30, 2026, net loss was $68.2 million, or a loss of $2.35 per diluted common share, compared to a net loss of $81.9 million, or a loss of $3.84 per diluted common share for the corresponding period in 2025.

 

Enanta’s cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities totaled $194.5 million at September 30, 2026. Enanta expects that its current cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities, as well as its retained portion of future royalty revenue, will be sufficient to meet the anticipated cash requirements of its existing business and development programs into fiscal 2029.

 

Virology

Enanta’s virology pipeline includes the leading portfolio of RSV treatments in clinical development, consisting of zelicapavir, a once-daily N-protein inhibitor, and EDP-323, a once-daily L-protein inhibitor, both of which received Fast Track designation from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration.

•
Enrollment is completed in LOTUS, a Phase 2b trial of zelicapavir in pediatric patients with RSV. LOTUS is designed to evaluate time to complete resolution of RSV signs and symptoms as measured by the RESOLVE-P clinical scoring scale, together with the safety and antiviral activity of zelicapavir. Topline data from LOTUS are expected in the first quarter of 2027.

 

 

•
Enrollment is ongoing in RESOLVE, a Phase 2b/3 trial of zelicapavir in adults at high-risk for severe RSV disease, including patients aged 75 years or older and those with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease or congestive heart failure. The trial will evaluate time to complete resolution of 13 RSV symptoms as measured by RiiQ™, while secondary endpoints include reduction in hospitalization rate, additional clinical efficacy measures, virology and safety. Topline data from the Phase 2b portion of RESOLVE are expected in 2027.

 

Immunology

Enanta’s immunology pipeline is focused on designing and developing highly potent and selective oral inhibitors for the treatment of inflammatory diseases by targeting key drivers of the type 2 immune response.

•
Enanta’s lead immunology candidate, EDP-978, is a potent and selective, oral once-daily KIT inhibitor in development for the treatment of chronic urticaria and potentially other mast cell-mediated diseases. A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, first-in-human Phase 1 clinical trial is ongoing and the Company expects to complete dosing by year-end 2026. Enanta now expects to report topline data from the trial in the first quarter of 2027.
•
Enanta’s second immunology candidate, EDP-903, is a potent and selective, oral once-daily STAT6 inhibitor in development for the treatment of atopic dermatitis and other diseases currently treated by dupilumab. While IND-enabling activities are complete, due to a delay in drug manufacturing, the Company is now targeting an IND filing in the second quarter of 2027.
•
Enanta’s third immunology program targets MRGPRX2, a non-canonical G-Protein-Coupled-Receptor (GPCR) expressed predominantly on mast cells, as well as peripheral neurons. Inhibiting MRGPRX2 may have potential to address multiple mast-cell driven diseases, as well as migraine. The Company selected EPS-5336 as a development candidate. EPS-5336 is a potent inhibitor of MRGPRX2 signaling in vitro and in vivo, with sub-nanomolar potency (EC50 of 0.2-0.6nM) in cellular assays via the preferred noncompetitive mechanism of inhibition. EPS-5336 also prevents skin mast cell activation in humanized MRGPRX2 mouse models. Further, EPS-5336 shows potent activity across multiple MRGPRX2 agonists and inhibits MRGPRX2-induced degranulation in migraine-relevant meningeal mast cells. EPS-5336 is highly selective for MRGPRX2 versus other GPCRs. EPS-5336 also demonstrates favorable in vitro and in vivo ADME properties, supportive of once-daily dosing potential. The Company expects to file an IND in the fourth quarter of 2027.

 

 

Corporate

•
In October 2026, Enanta announced that Jay R. Luly, Ph.D., age 70, will retire as President and Chief Executive Officer, by February 1, 2027, following 23 years of leadership with the Company. The Board of Directors has initiated a comprehensive search for Dr. Luly's successor, considering both internal and external candidates. Dr. Luly is expected to continue to serve as a strategic advisor for the remainder of 2027 to support a smooth and orderly transition. The Company also announced the appointment of Tara L. Kieffer, Ph.D., to the newly created role of Chief Operating Officer. After a 16-year career of advancement at Vertex Pharmaceuticals in research, clinical development, integrated program management and business development, Dr. Kieffer joined Enanta in 2020, most recently serving as the Company’s Chief Product Strategy Officer.
•
In August 2025, Enanta filed a patent infringement action in the Unified Patent Court (UPC) of the European Union against Pfizer Inc. and certain of its subsidiaries, seeking a determination of liability for infringement of European Patent No. EP 4 051 265 (the ’265 Patent) in connection with Pfizer’s manufacture, use, and sale of Paxlovid™ (nirmatrelvir tablets; ritonavir tablets) in the 18 EU member states participating in the UPC. Pfizer counterclaimed for revocation of the ’265 Patent. A hearing on the infringement action and counterclaim for revocation was held in September 2026. In October, the UPC released its decision concluding that the ’265 Patent is invalid. The UPC also determined that the infringement action was unfounded in light of its decision on validity. In the separate opposition proceeding filed by Pfizer with the European Patent Office (EPO) against the ’265 Patent, the EPO has set a hearing date in April 2027.
•
In September, Enanta conducted a strategic assessment of its resource allocation and operating structure to ensure alignment with its current priorities and long-term objectives. As a result of this review, the Company implemented targeted workforce reductions across the organization to increase operational efficiency, focus resources on its highest-priority opportunities and extend financial flexibility. In connection with these actions, Enanta’s workforce was reduced by approximately one-third.

Enanta will present at the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference in London on November 18, 2026, and at the 9th Annual Evercore Healthcare Conference on December 2, 2026.
•
Enanta plans to issue its fiscal first quarter 2027 financial results press release on February 8, 2027.

 

About Enanta Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

Enanta is using its robust, chemistry-driven approach and drug discovery capabilities to become a leader in the discovery and development of small molecule drugs for viral infections and immunological diseases. In virology, Enanta’s clinical programs are focused on the development of first-in-disease and best-in-disease treatments for respiratory syncytial virus (RSV). The Company’s immunology pipeline aims to develop treatments for inflammatory diseases by targeting key drivers of the type 2 immune response, with KIT, STAT6 and MRGPRX2 inhibition.

 

 

 

Glecaprevir, a protease inhibitor discovered by Enanta, is part of the only treatment approved for curing both acute and chronic hepatitis C virus (HCV) infection and is sold by AbbVie in numerous countries under the tradenames MAVYRET® (U.S.) and MAVIRET® (ex-U.S.) (glecaprevir/pibrentasvir). A portion of Enanta’s royalties from HCV products developed under its collaboration with AbbVie contribute ongoing funding to Enanta’s operations. Please visit www.enanta.com for more information.
