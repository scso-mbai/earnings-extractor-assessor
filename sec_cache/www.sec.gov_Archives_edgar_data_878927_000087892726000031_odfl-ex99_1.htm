Marty Freeman, President and Chief Executive Officer of Old Dominion, commented, “Old Dominion produced strong financial results in the third quarter that included a 13.1% increase in revenue and a 28.9% increase in our earnings per diluted share. These results were made possible by the hard work and dedication of our OD Family of employees who continued to diligently execute on the core aspects of our long-term strategic plan. The cornerstone of our strategic plan remains our commitment to providing our customers with superior service at a fair price, and we were pleased that our service metrics were once again best-in-class in the third quarter with 99% on-time service and a claims ratio of 0.1%.

“Our 13.1% increase in revenue was primarily due to a 13.3% increase in our LTL revenue per hundredweight that was partially offset by a 0.1% decrease in our LTL tons per day. The decrease in our LTL tons per day reflects the net impact of a 2.0% decrease in our LTL shipments per day and a 1.9% increase in our LTL weight per shipment. Demand for our service improved during the quarter and our LTL tons per day increased on a year-over-year basis in September. LTL revenue per hundredweight, excluding fuel surcharges, increased 5.1% compared to the third quarter of 2025 as we continued to maintain our disciplined approach to yield management.

“Our operating ratio improved by 280 basis points to 71.5% for the third quarter of 2026. Within our direct operating costs, improvement in our salaries, wages and benefits as a percentage of revenue more than offset an increase in our

The combination of revenue growth and operating ratio improvement resulted in a 28.9% increase in our earnings per diluted share to $1.65 for the third quarter.”

Cash Flow and Use of Capital

Old Dominion’s net cash provided by operating activities was $469.5 million for the third quarter of 2026 and $1.1 billion for the first nine months of this year. The Company had $466.2 million in cash and cash equivalents at September 30, 2026.

Capital expenditures were $67.1 million for the third quarter of 2026 and $206.7 million for the first nine months of this year. The Company expects its aggregate capital expenditures for 2026 to total approximately $380 million, including planned expenditures of $180 million for real estate and service center expansion projects; $155 million for tractors and trailers; and $45 million for information technology and other assets.

Old Dominion continued to return capital to shareholders during the third quarter of 2026 through its share repurchase and dividend programs. For the first nine months of this year, the Company utilized $400.6 million of cash for its share repurchase program and paid $180.7 million in cash dividends.

Summary

Mr. Freeman concluded, “Old Dominion produced another quarter of strong, profitable revenue growth. Our unwavering commitment to providing our customers with the highest standard of service has created an unmatched value proposition in our industry and supports our ongoing focus on revenue quality. As this quarter demonstrates, our consistent investments in our network, our people and our technology provide us with the flexibility to respond quickly to changes in the demand environment. This flexibility allows us to support our customers with the capacity that they require while continuing to operate efficiently. As a result, we remain confident in our ability to win profitable market share and increase shareholder value over the long term.”

Old Dominion Freight Line, Inc. is one of the largest North American LTL motor carriers and provides regional, inter-regional and national LTL services through a single integrated, union-free organization. Our service offerings, which include expedited transportation, are provided through an expansive network of service centers located throughout the continental United States. Through strategic alliances, we also provide LTL services throughout North America. In addition to our core LTL services, we offer a range of value-added services including container drayage, truckload brokerage and supply chain consulting.
