EXHIBIT 99.1

 

 

Inuvo Third Quarter 2026 Results Reflect Inflection Point as IntentKey Pipeline Builds

 

IntentKey’s Privacy-By-Design Value Proposition Resonates as Sales Pipeline Builds

 

Legacy Search Revenue Stabilizes

 

Management to host conference call at 4:15 PM ET, Thursday, November 12, 2026

 

LITTLE ROCK, AR, November 12, 2026 – Inuvo, Inc. (NYSE American: INUV), a provider of artificial intelligence-driven solutions for modeling media audiences, today announced financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

 

Recent Business Highlights

 

 

·

 Audience Modeling Revenue Down While Pipeline Builds: Third quarter Audience Modeling revenue was 24% lower year-over-year while our sales pipeline expanded across automotive, travel, healthcare and financial verticals, reflecting the dynamics of our strategic pivot.

 

·

 Legacy Search Stabilization: Strategic moves to rationalize Legacy Search results in stabilized revenue streams as Legacy Search covers its direct operating costs.

 

·

 Expanded Workflow Integration: Completed IntentKey MCP integration with Anthropic’s Claude, enabling seamless AI and agentic workflow integration for marketers.

 

·

 White-Label Partner Program Launched: Opened a new route to market by expanding IntentKey to include partner program that enables agency partners to offer IntentKey under their own brand.

 

·

 Uninterrupted Service Through Apple iOS Updates: IntentKey continued to perform for clients on Safari as Apple's latest iOS update continues to choke identity-based ad delivery, significantly impacting competing adtechs.

 

·

 Enhanced IntentKey Dynamic Targeting: Launched privacy-first neighborhood targeting that identifies where interest is rising and updates hourly, enabling IntentKey to reach CTV audiences without user-level identifiers.

 

“Our third quarter performance marks a decisive inflection point,” said Rob Buchner, Chairman and CEO of Inuvo. “With Legacy Search stabilized after several quarters of negative cash flow, we are accelerating into an advertising environment that urgently requires privacy-first AI solutions. As legacy ad tech platforms struggle to adapt to industry turbulence, demand is concentrating around IntentKey’s core strengths: privacy, precision, speed, and proven discovery of net-new audiences. During the fourth quarter, we are executing aggressively our plan to close our growing sales pipeline, grow our client base, and embed our data into next-generation agentic workflows.”

Financial Results for the Third Quarter 2026

 

Net revenue decreased 70% to $6.8 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared with the same period in 2025, led by an 80% year-over-year decline in Legacy Search revenue resulting from structural changes in the industry and the reset of the Company’s Bonfire platform. Third quarter 2026 Audience Modeling revenue decreased 24% compared with the third quarter of 2025, reflecting lower spend from a key client. In addition, the Company overhauled its sales team and go-to-market strategy in early summer 2026, which has resulted in an expanding sales pipeline, but fewer revenue conversions during the quarter as sales progress through the typical sales cycle of four-to-six months.

 

Third quarter 2026 gross margin was 44%, compared with 73% in the third quarter of 2025. The change was driven by the contraction in Legacy Search and the resulting shift in revenue mix. Legacy Search has historically carried a higher gross margin but a lower direct operating margin than Audience Modeling, as a lower proportion of its costs are recorded in cost of revenue and a greater portion in marketing and other operating expenses.

 

Third quarter 2026 operating expenses were $7.8 million and included a $2.4 million non-cash charge related to the impairment of certain intangible and long-lived assets related to Legacy Search. Operating expenses were also impacted by a 90% year-over-year decrease in marketing costs attributable to lower traffic acquisition costs associated with Legacy Search and a 35% year-over-year decrease in compensation costs related to cost optimization efforts made earlier in the year. Operating loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $4.8 million compared with $1.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Financing expense, net was $0.4 million compared with $0.1 million in the prior-year period, reflecting the secured promissory notes issued in June 2026. Other income of $0.3 million reflected a net gain on the change in fair value of notes payable.

 

Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $5.0 million, or $0.28 per share, compared with a net loss of $1.7 million, or $0.12 per share, in the same period in 2025. Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was a loss of $1.6 million, compared with a loss of $0.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

Liquidity and Capital Resources

 

During the quarter, the Company completed the purchase and sale of common stock and pre-funded warrants in a registered direct offering as well as a concurrent private placement of warrants for gross proceeds of $3.0 million. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $1.0 million in cash and cash equivalents and $6.2 million in restricted cash. The Company will need to raise additional capital, continue implementing cost reductions, and expand revenue to fund its operations over the next twelve months.

2026 Outlook

 

For the balance of 2026, Inuvo currently expects:

 

 

·

 Audience Modeling Growth – Fourth quarter revenue expected to increase compared to third quarter 2026 supported by a healthy sales pipeline.

 

·

 Legacy Search – Quarterly revenue and margins in line with third quarter 2026.

 

·

 Operating Discipline – Fourth quarter operating expenses expected to remain lower year over year as a result of lower compensation costs and lower traffic acquisition costs, consistent with lower Legacy Search revenue.

 

·

 Strategic Execution - Continued focus on brand-direct growth, raising IntentKey’s industry profile, product innovation and higher-margin revenue.

About Inuvo

 

Inuvo, Inc. (NYSE American: INUV) is a disruptive AI specifically designed for modeling media audiences. IntentKey® AI is a patented technology capable of identifying customer engagement based on real-time media consumption. Our models refresh every 5 minutes and know, with precision, why prospects are interested in a product or brand, in turn, predicting purchase intent 24 hours before legacy programmatic systems can respond to buying signals. Reconciliation of Net Loss to EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA

 

We present EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA as a supplemental measure of our performance. We defined EBITDA as net income/(loss) plus (i) financing expense, (ii) income tax expense, (iii) depreciation, and (iv) amortization. We further define Adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA plus (v) stock-based compensation and ( vi) change in fair value of notes payable and loss on extinguishment of debt, which are non-cash and result from financing activities rather than operations, (vii) impairment, which is non-cash, (viii) the settlement agreement, which relates to a specific matter outside our normal operations, and (ix) severance, which relates to workforce reductions rather than ongoing operating costs. These adjustments are itemized below. We use EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA internally in analyzing our financial results and believe they are useful to investors, as a supplement to GAAP measures, in evaluating our operational performance. You are encouraged to evaluate these adjustments and the reasons we consider them appropriate for supplemental analysis. In evaluating EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA, you should be aware that in the future we may incur expenses that are the same or similar to some of the adjustments in the presentation. Our presentation of EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as an inference that our future results will be unaffected by unusual or non-recurring items.
