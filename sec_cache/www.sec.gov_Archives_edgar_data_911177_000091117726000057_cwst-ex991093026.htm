CASELLA WASTE SYSTEMS, INC. ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS
RUTLAND, VERMONT (November 9, 2026) — Casella Waste Systems, Inc. (Nasdaq: CWST), a regional solid waste, recycling and resource management services company, today reported its financial results for the three and nine month periods ended September 30, 2026.
Key Highlights:
•Revenues were $559.2 million for the quarter, up $73.9 million, or up 15.2%, from the same period in 2025.
•Solid waste pricing for the quarter was up 5.5% from the same period in 2025, driven by 5.9% collection price growth and 4.8% disposal price growth.
•Net loss was $(7.0) million for the quarter, as compared to net income of $10.0 million for the same period in 2025, impacted by an impairment charge described below. Adjusted Net Income, a non-GAAP measure, was $22.3 million for the quarter, down $(5.1) million from the same period in 2025.
•Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP measure, was $133.5 million for the quarter, up $13.6 million, or up 11.4%, from the same period in 2025.
•Net cash provided by operating activities was $241.8 million for the year-to-date period, up $8.6 million, or up 3.7%, from the same period in 2025.
•Adjusted Free Cash Flow, a non-GAAP measure, was $111.1 million for the year-to-date period, down $(8.4) million, or down (7.0)%, from the same period in 2025.
•Acquired six businesses in the first nine months of 2026 with over $170 million in aggregate annualized revenues.
•Narrowed 2026 guidance for Adjusted EBITDA, reaffirmed guidance for Adjusted Free Cash Flow and raised guidance for revenues.
“We again delivered solid top-line revenue and Adjusted EBITDA growth in the third quarter with strong performance across most of our business,” said Ned Coletta, President and CEO of Casella Waste Systems, Inc. “Solid waste pricing was robust, up 5.9% in collection and up 4.6% at our landfills, along with 12.8% landfill volume growth. Where fully implemented, floating fuel fees continued to insulate our business from the impact of elevated and volatile fuel prices. We remain highly confident in our operating plans for this year, supported by strong fourth quarter performance visibility, and as such have narrowed our 2026 guidance on Adjusted EBITDA and maintained our Adjusted Free Cash Flow guidance, while raising our revenue guidance to reflect higher floating fuel fees.”
“During the third quarter in our Mid-Atlantic region, we moved aggressively to strengthen regional leadership and provide corporate support to accelerate operational initiatives, including route consolidation and efficiencies, rolling out floating fuel fees consistent with our companywide programs, and implementing price increases to offset cost inflation,” Coletta said. “Although our results in the Mid-Atlantic were slightly behind plan in the quarter, we have seen a definitive positive inflection point in the region entering the fourth quarter, with October Mid-Atlantic Adjusted EBITDA margins up approximately 150 basis points from August and up over 300 basis points year-over-year. While meaningful work remains, we expect these targeted actions to drive sustained regional performance gains through the remainder of the year and into 2027.”
Coletta added, “We continued to make significant progress on our multi-year G&A cost reduction and margin expansion objectives, completing the initial steps of our planned organizational transformation in late September and implementing credit card convenience fees throughout the quarter, with cost reduction benefits of both efforts expected to be reflected in the fourth quarter. We believe we are well positioned for strong growth in 2027 and a minimum of 300 basis points of Adjusted EBITDA margin expansion over the next three years.”
Q3 2026 Results
Revenues were $559.2 million for the quarter, up $73.9 million, or up 15.2%, from the same period in 2025, with revenue growth mainly driven by: the positive impact from acquisitions, including the rollover contribution from deals closed in prior periods; positive collection and disposal pricing; an increase in landfill volumes; and strong National Accounts growth in our Resource Solutions operating segment.
Operating income was $15.6 million for the quarter, down $(13.7) million from the same period in 2025, reflecting higher expenses including depreciation and amortization, an impairment charge related to our termination of the Granite State landfill development project, expenses related to acquisition activities and organizational transformation costs.

Net loss was $(7.0) million for the quarter, as compared to net income of $10.0 million for the same period in 2025, largely driven by the same factors impacting operating income in addition to higher interest expense, net, partially offset by the monetization of investment tax credits. Adjusted Net Income was $22.3 million for the quarter, down $(5.1) million, or down (18.7)%, from the same period in 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA was $133.5 million for the quarter, up $13.6 million, or up 11.4%, from the same period in 2025, driven by both acquisition contribution and organic growth.

Fiscal Year 2026 Outlook
The Company updated guidance for the fiscal year ending December 31, 2026 (“fiscal year 2026”) for the following ranges:
•Revenues between $2.100 billion and $2.110 billion (raised from a range of $2.090 billion to $2.110 billion);
•Adjusted EBITDA between $475 million and $481 million (narrowed from between $473 million and $483 million); and
•Net loss between $(10) and $(4) million (lowered from a range of net income between $0 million and $6 million).
The Company reaffirmed guidance for fiscal year 2026 by estimating results in the following ranges:
•Net cash provided by operating activities between $370 million and $380 million; and
•Adjusted Free Cash Flow between $200 million and $210 million.
The guidance ranges do not include the impact of any acquisitions that have not been completed. Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Free Cash Flow related to fiscal year 2026 are described in the Unaudited Reconciliation of Fiscal Year 2026 Outlook Non-GAAP Measures section of this press release. Net loss and Net cash provided by operating activities are provided as the most directly comparable GAAP measures to Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Free Cash Flow, respectively, however these forward-looking estimates for fiscal year 2026 do not contemplate any unanticipated impacts.

(i)The Company's capital expenditures are broadly defined as pertaining to either growth or replacement activities. Growth capital expenditures are defined as costs related to development projects, organic business growth, and the integration of newly acquired operations. Growth capital expenditures include costs related to the following: 1) acquisition capital expenditures that are necessary to transition and upgrade acquired assets to Company operating standards and to achieve strategic synergies associated with integrating newly acquired operations, which can be considered, together with acquisition purchase price, as part of the initial overall investment in an acquired business; 2) McKean Landfill rail capital expenditures, which is unique and different from landfill construction investments in the normal course of operations because the Company is investing in long-term infrastructure; and 3) development of landfill permit expansions, investment in infrastructure to increase throughput at transfer stations and recycling and other processing facilities, capital expenditures for new equipment, such as trucks, containers or compactors, to support new contracts or other organic business growth, and other development projects in support of our growth strategies. Replacement capital expenditures are defined as landfill cell construction costs not related to expansion airspace, costs for normal permit renewals, replacement costs for equipment and other capital expenditures due to age or obsolescence, and capital items not otherwise defined as growth capital expenditures.
