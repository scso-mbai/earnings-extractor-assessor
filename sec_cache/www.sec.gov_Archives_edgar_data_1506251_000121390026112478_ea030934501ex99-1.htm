Citius Oncology Reports Continued Institutional Demand and 120% Growth in Shipments of LYMPHIR® Units to U.S. Wholesalers During the Fiscal Fourth Quarter of 2026

 

CRANFORD, N.J., October 26, 2026 - Citius Oncology, Inc. (“Citius Oncology” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: CTOR), an oncology-focused biopharmaceutical company and majority-owned subsidiary of Citius Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (“Citius Pharma”) (Nasdaq: CTXR), today announced preliminary, unaudited LYMPHIR® (denileukin diftitox-cxdl) unit sales to U.S. wholesalers and institutional vial orders for the fiscal fourth quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“The fiscal fourth quarter showed continued institutional demand for LYMPHIR. Unit sales to wholesalers more than doubled as our expanded commercial team reached nationwide coverage in August,” said Leonard Mazur, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Citius Oncology. “We are now more than halfway to our goal of having LYMPHIR on formulary at 100 priority institutions by calendar year-end. As we enter fiscal 2027, we expect the momentum we built in the fourth quarter to carry forward, with growing institutional demand, deeper penetration into academic and community treatment centers, and continued sales growth as LYMPHIR becomes further established in CTCL.”

 

Preliminary Fiscal Fourth Quarter 2026 Highlights (unaudited)

 

●792 units purchased by U.S. wholesalers for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to 360 units for the three months ended June 30, 2026, an increase of 120%;

 

●950 LYMPHIR vials ordered by prescribing centers from wholesalers during the fiscal fourth quarter, compared to 926 for the fiscal third quarter; and

 

●As of the date of this release, 54 institutions have now ordered LYMPHIR, compared to 44 as of August 14, 2026, as last reported.

 

The unit information in this release is preliminary and unaudited, is based on management’s current estimates, and is subject to completion of the Company’s year-end closing procedures and the audit of its fiscal 2026 financial statements. Actual results may differ, and the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm has not audited, reviewed or performed any procedures on these preliminary figures. The Company recognizes revenue when wholesaler orders are fulfilled. Accordingly, the Company’s revenue is based on LYMPHIR units sold to wholesalers, net of estimated gross-to-net adjustments, rather than on institutional vial orders from wholesalers by institutions. Citius Oncology expects to report complete fiscal fourth quarter and fiscal year 2026 results in December 2026 with the filing of its Annual Report on Form 10-K.

 

About LYMPHIR® (denileukin diftitox-cxdl)

 

LYMPHIR is a targeted immunotherapy for relapsed or refractory cutaneous T-cell lymphoma (CTCL) indicated for use in Stage I–III disease after at least one prior systemic therapy. It is a recombinant fusion protein that combines the IL-2 receptor binding domain with diphtheria toxin (DT) fragments. The agent specifically binds to IL-2 receptors on the cell surface, causing DT fragments that have entered cells to inhibit protein synthesis, resulting in cell death. Denileukin diftitox-cxdl has demonstrated the ability to deplete immunosuppressive regulatory T lymphocytes (Tregs) and antitumor activity through a direct cytocidal action on IL-2R-expressing tumors. LYMPHIR was approved by the FDA and subsequently launched in the U.S. in December 2025.

 

 

 

About Citius Oncology, Inc.

 

Citius Oncology, Inc. (Nasdaq: CTOR) is a platform to develop and commercialize novel targeted oncology therapies. In December 2025, Citius Oncology launched LYMPHIR, approved by the FDA for the treatment of adults with relapsed or refractory Stage I–III CTCL after at least one prior systemic therapy. Management estimates the initial CTCL market for LYMPHIR currently exceeds $400 million, is growing, and is underserved by existing therapies. About Citius Pharmaceuticals, Inc.

 

Citius Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Nasdaq: CTXR) is a biopharmaceutical company dedicated to the development and commercialization of first-in-class critical care products. Citius Pharma is the majority owner of Citius Oncology. In December 2025, Citius Oncology launched LYMPHIR, a targeted immunotherapy for the treatment of adults with relapsed or refractory Stage I–III CTCL who had had at least one prior systemic therapy. Citius Pharma’s late-stage pipeline also includes Mino-Lok®, a catheter lock solution to salvage catheters in patients with catheter-related bloodstream infections, and CITI-002 (Halo-Lido), a topical formulation for the relief of hemorrhoids. A pivotal Phase 3 trial for Mino-Lok and a Phase 2b trial for Halo-Lido were completed in 2023. Mino-Lok met primary and secondary endpoints of its Phase 3 trial. Citius Pharma is actively engaged with the FDA to outline next steps for both programs. For more information, please visit www.citiuspharma.com.
