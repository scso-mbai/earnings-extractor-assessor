Aptera Motors Reports Third Quarter and First Nine Months 2026 Financial Results

 

November 13, 2026

 

●Reports Q3 2026 GAAP Net Loss of $14.0 Million and First Nine Months 2026 GAAP Net Loss of $35.0 Million
●Reports Q3 2026 Adjusted Net Loss of $10.2 Million and First Nine Months 2026 Adjusted Net Loss of $23.5 Million (Non-GAAP)
●Ended Quarter with $9.2 Million in Cash and Cash Equivalents; Raised Approximately $6.0 Million in Gross Proceeds During the Quarter in a Warrant Inducement Transaction
●Issued Purchase Orders for Bodies and Chassis for Initial Production Vehicles and Entered into a Strategic Partnership Agreement with Shanghai Launch Automotive Technology
●Raised Approximately $23.1 Million in Gross Proceeds in the First Nine Months of 2026 Through a Public Offering and Warrant Inducements and Exercises, Plus Approximately $2.3 Million in Net Proceeds Under the Equity Line of Credit

 

CARLSBAD, Calif., November 13, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Aptera Motors Corp. (NASDAQ: SEV) (“Aptera” or the “Company”), a solar mobility company advancing ultra-efficient transportation, today reported its financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

“In the third quarter we started the process of procuring parts for our first production vehicles. We also entered into a strategic partnership with Launch to support manufacturing and contract engineering, which we believe has helped lower our estimated capital needs to reach start of production and, most importantly, the capital we estimate is required to reach a production rate at which we believe we could fund ongoing operations from vehicle sales. Research and development spending more than doubled from the prior-year quarter as we directed our resources to vehicle validation and production readiness,” said Chris Anthony, Co-CEO of Aptera.

Business Update

 

During the third quarter of 2026, the Company announced a partnership with RepairPal to provide certified service for its customers nationwide, and TÜV Rheinland independently measured more than 4 kilowatt-hours of usable solar energy delivered to the vehicle’s battery on each of three test days. In August 2026, the Company ordered major parts for its initial production vehicles.

 

In August 2026, the Company entered into a Strategic Partnership Agreement with Shanghai Launch Automotive Technology Co., Ltd. (“Launch”), under which Launch will serve as a manufacturing and contract-engineering partner and perform manufacturing, engineering, tooling, testing, and related work. The agreement covers up to RMB 300 million in services, two-thirds payable in cash and one-third through warrants to purchase Class B Common Stock. The Company plans to begin building and delivering production vehicles as soon as practicable, subject to timely financing and regulatory approvals.

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had more than 49,000 reservations for its vehicle.

 

Liquidity

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $9.2 million in cash and cash equivalents, including $4.1 million of refundable reservation fees collected from reservation holders.

 

During the first nine months of 2026, the Company raised approximately $23.1 million in gross proceeds through a public offering and warrant inducements and exercises, plus approximately $2.3 million in net proceeds under its equity line of credit. Since quarter end, the Company has raised approximately $6.0 million in gross proceeds through a registered direct offering and concurrent private placement, and approximately $0.6 million in net proceeds under its equity line of credit and at-the-market facility. The Company currently estimates it will need approximately $25 million to reach start of series production, including certain tooling and equipment, and approximately $65 million in total to reach a production rate of 500 vehicles per month, subject to obtaining timely financing on acceptable terms, supplier performance and regulatory approvals. The Company intends to use the net proceeds from the November 2026 registered direct offering toward these requirements.

 

The Company’s existing cash is not sufficient to fund its operations for the next twelve months, and substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going concern has not been alleviated. Additional details regarding the Company’s going concern assessment are provided in the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

 
 

 

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

This press release includes Adjusted Net Loss and Adjusted Net Loss Per Share, which are non-GAAP financial measures. We define Adjusted Net Loss as GAAP net loss, excluding (i) non-cash stock-based compensation expense and (ii) the litigation settlement charge related to the Company’s previously disclosed litigation with Zaptera USA, Inc., which was settled and dismissed with prejudice in April 2026. We believe that these non-GAAP measures, when viewed in conjunction with our GAAP results, provide a more complete understanding of our core operating performance and trends, as these adjustments remove non-cash expenses and a discrete, non-recurring charge related to the settlement of the Zaptera litigation.

 

These non-GAAP measures are presented in addition to, and not as a substitute for, GAAP results. Non-GAAP measures have material limitations and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. We encourage investors to review these measures together with our GAAP results and the reconciliations provided.

About Aptera Motors Corp.

 

Aptera Motors Corp. (Nasdaq: SEV) is a solar mobility company driven by a mission to advance the future of efficient transportation. Its flagship vehicle is conceived to be a paradigm-shifting solar electric vehicle that leverages breakthroughs in aerodynamics, material science, and solar technology to pursue new levels of efficiency. As a public benefit corporation, Aptera is committed to building a sustainable business that positively impacts its stakeholders and the environment. Aptera is headquartered in Carlsbad, California. For more information, please visit www.aptera.us.
