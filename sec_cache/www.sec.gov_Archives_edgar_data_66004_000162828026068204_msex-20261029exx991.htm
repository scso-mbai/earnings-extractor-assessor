Middlesex Water Company Reports Third Quarter 2026 Earnings
•Diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) of $0.95
•Invested $84 million in water and wastewater utility infrastructure during the nine months ended September 30, 2026
•Declared $0.38 common stock cash dividend, a 5.56% increase

ISELIN, N.J., October 29, 2026 -- Middlesex Water Company (“Middlesex” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: MSEX) today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
“Our strong third-quarter results reflect increased wholesale demand, higher customer consumption, and the continued execution of our regulatory strategy,” said Nadine Leslie, Chair, President, and Chief Executive Officer of Middlesex Water Company. “As we deliver solid earnings growth, we remain focused on investing in critical water and wastewater infrastructure, strengthening system reliability, and pursuing selective and sustainable growth under our MWC 2030 Vision to create long-term value for customers and shareholders.”

Third Quarter 2026 Results
Middlesex reported net income of $18.0 million and diluted EPS of $0.95, compared to net income of $14.0 million and diluted EPS of $0.77 for the same quarter in 2025.
Operating revenues for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, were $60.2 million, an increase of $6.1 million, compared to the same quarter last year. Operating revenues for the Middlesex System increased $4.7 million due to increased wholesale demand, customer consumption and base rate increases. Tidewater System revenues increased $1.2 million due to increased customer consumption, customer growth and rate increases. Non-regulated revenues increased $0.2 million, primarily due to higher supplemental contract services.
Operating expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, were $37.9 million, an increase of approximately $1.6 million, compared to the same quarter last year. Operations and maintenance expenses increased $1.1 million primarily due to increases in variable production costs from higher customer demand and employee related costs, partially offset by higher capitalizable costs; and other taxes increased $0.5 million due to higher gross receipts taxes on higher revenues in the Middlesex System. Depreciation expense decreased $0.1 million from the same period in 2025 due to the implementation of depreciation rates from the study approved by the New Jersey Board of Public Utilities ("NJBPU"), partially offset by higher average utility plant in service.
Year-to-Date Results
Net income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was $43.4 million, a $9.2 million increase when compared to the same period in 2025. Diluted earnings per share were $2.31 and $1.90 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, and 2025, respectively.
Operating revenues for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, were $165.3 million, an increase of approximately $17.6 compared to the same period in 2025. Middlesex System revenues increased $13.5 million due to increased wholesale demand, customer consumption and base rate increases. Tidewater System revenues increased $3.8 million due to increased customer consumption, customer growth, and rate increases. Non-regulated revenues increased $0.3 million, primarily due to higher supplemental contract services.


Operating expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, increased $7.1 million compared to the same period in 2025. Operations and maintenance expenses increased $5.4 million primarily due to increases in allowance for credit loss reserve, employee related costs and variable production costs from higher customer demand, partially offset by higher capitalizable costs. Depreciation expense increased $0.5 million due to higher average utility plant in service, partially offset by the implementation of depreciation rates from the study approved by the NJBPU. Other taxes increased $1.3 million due to higher gross receipts taxes on higher revenues in the Middlesex System.
Selective and Sustainable Growth
Middlesex invested approximately $84 million to upgrade, replace and enhance the resiliency of its water and wastewater utility infrastructure during the nine months ended September 30, 2026, or approximately 67% of its planned 2026 annual investment of approximately $126 million.
In September 2026, Middlesex filed Resiliency and Environmental System Improvement Charge and Distribution System Improvement Charge petitions with the NJBPU requesting $0.8 million and $1.7 million of additional annual revenues, respectively.
In October 2026, the Delaware Public Service Commission approved the joint application by Tidewater Utilities, Inc. ("Tidewater") and Mount Pleasant MHP, LLC ("Mount Pleasant") for Tidewater to purchase the water utility assets of Mount Pleasant for $25 thousand. Mount Pleasant serves 39 customers in New Castle County, Delaware.
Dividend Declared, 5.56% Increase Announced
On October 23, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors declared a fourth quarter cash dividend of $0.38 per common share, a 5.56% increase from the $0.36 per share dividend last declared in July 2026. This increase raises the annual dividend rate to $1.52 from $1.44 per share of common stock and marks Middlesex's 54th consecutive year of dividend increases. The dividend at the new rate is payable December 1, 2026, to shareholders of record as of November 16, 2026. Middlesex has paid cash dividends each year since 1912.
About Middlesex Water Company
Middlesex Water Company (“Middlesex”) is one of the nation’s premier investor-owned water and wastewater utilities. Established in 1897, Middlesex is a trusted provider of life-sustaining services to more than half a million people in New Jersey and Delaware. The company focuses on employee engagement, operational excellence, superior customer experience, investment in infrastructure, and selective and sustainable growth to deliver value to our customers, investors, and the communities we serve.
