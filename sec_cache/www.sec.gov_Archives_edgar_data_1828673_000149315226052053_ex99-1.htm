HCW Biologics Reports Third Quarter 2026

Business and Clinical Development Highlights and Financial Results

 

Miramar, FL – November 16, 2026 – HCW Biologics Inc. (the “Company” or “HCW Biologics”) (NASDAQ: HCWB), a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on developing transformative fusion immunotherapeutics to treat autoimmune diseases, cancer and senescence-associated dysplasia, today reported financial results and recent business highlights for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

The Company featured two of its preclinical, non-Fc TRBC molecules at the 41st Annual Meeting of the Society for Immunotherapy of Cancer (“SITC2026”) which took place from November 4 – 8, 2026 in Phoenix, Arizona. In addition, the Company showcased the uniqueness of its TRBC platform to create novel multivalent non-Fc fusion molecules for solid tumor and senescence-associated diseases therapies.

 

The submission for the Company’s second-generation, tetravalent immune checkpoint inhibitor, HCW11-040, was recognized by SITC2026 as one of the top 150 abstracts out of over 1,200 abstracts submitted for its scientific rigor, novelty, clarity and potential impact on cancer immunotherapy. In the Poster presentation of its T Cell Engager Program, the Company shared the newly discovered, distinctive features of its lead product candidate in this program, HCW11-018b, including capabilities to induce/promote tertiary lymphoid structure formation inside tumors, convert immunological “cold” tumors into “hot” tumors, and enhance T-cell activation and tumor infiltration while mitigating immunosuppression, using subcutaneous (“s.c.”) administration.

 

In the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company activated an additional clinical site to participate in the HCW9302 Phase 1 clinical study in alopecia areata and continued patient enrollment and treatment in the third dose cohort (i.e., eight (8) micrograms/kg body weight). In patients treated to date, the s.c. administration of HCW9302 has been well-tolerated and has had no dose-limiting toxicities. With continued patient enrollment, the Phase 1 readout and determination of the Recommended Phase 2 Dose are expected in the first quarter of 2027. The Company intends to present the full results of the Phase 1 clinical trial in an invited oral presentation at the 9th Treg-Directed Therapies Summit in March 2027 in Boston, Massachusetts.

 

Dr. Hing C. Wong, the Company’s Founder and Chief Executive Officer, stated, “Progress continues steadily for our clinical study evaluating HCW9302 in alopecia areata, and we are encouraged by results in humans that are consistent with our preclinical studies. We have the support of strong clinical sites who share our passion for developing breakthrough immunotherapeutics. With the addition of a third clinical site, we are hopeful that patient enrollment will maintain a good momentum as we come to the end of the Phase 1 clinical study. We are also excited about the great progress we have made with the molecules we have constructed using our TRBC platform, especially our lead T-cell engager, HCW11-018b, and our lead second-generation immune-checkpoint inhibitor, HCW11-040, which have the potential of revolutionizing treatment for solid tumors and senescence-associated diseases.”

 

 

 

 

Business and Clinical Development Highlights

 

In the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company raised $8.6 million of equity financing, which allowed it to continue to support a Phase 1 clinical study to evaluate HCW9302 in an autoimmune disease and advance preclinical programs, especially the Company’s T-Cell Engager Program. On November 4, 2026, the Company secured up to $10.0 million in cash through debt financing, and received $3.0 million, less transaction costs, at closing and access to an additional $7.0 million, subject to certain conditions. With this financing, the Company believes it has funding in place to reach the milestone of completion of the Phase 1 clinical trial for HCW9302 and determining the Recommended Phase 2 Dose level, which are expected in the first quarter of 2027.

 

As a result of discussions at a recent Type B meeting with the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (“FDA”), the FDA provided the Company with clear guidance for the next steps to prepare its IND application to support a first-in-human clinical study to evaluate HCW11-018b in solid tumors, which the Company intends to file in the first half of 2027. In support of potential upcoming clinical trials, the Company has initiated cGMP manufacturing of HCW11-018b. HCW Biologics has created a high-expression production cell line and a streamlined GMP manufacturing process, utilizing a relatively simple manufacturing process that is similar to the process used to make therapeutic monoclonal antibodies.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Revenues: Revenues for the three months ended September 30, 2025 and 2026 were $15,606 and $55,463, respectively. Revenues in the nine months ended September 30, 2025 and 2026 were $27,222 and $6.7 million, respectively. In the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025, at the request of the licensee, the Company agreed to suspend the license with AlloTera Therapeutics, Inc. (“AlloTera Therapeutics”), thus the Company recognized revenues related only to minor ancillary services such as storage. In the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company elected to terminate the license with AlloTera Therapeutics and recaptured the exclusive rights to the licensed molecules. In the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company completed the closing of the exclusive, worldwide licensing agreement with Beijing Trimmune Biotech Co., Ltd. (“Trimmune”) for the in vivo rights for HCW11-006 (“Trimmune License”) and performed additional post-transfer services under the agreement.

 

Research and development (R&D) expenses: R&D expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2025 and 2026 were $1.4 million and $1.2 million, respectively, a decrease of $161,670, or 12%. The decrease was primarily due to decreases in salaries, benefits and related taxes and preclinical expenses. R&D expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 and 2026 were $4.1 million and $3.7 million, respectively, a decrease of $405,905, or 10%. The decrease was primarily due to a decline in manufacturing and materials expenses, partially offset by increases in taxes and salaries, benefits and related expenses.

 

 

 

 

General and administrative (G&A) expenses: G&A expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2025 and 2026 were $1.8 million and $1.4 million, respectively, a decrease of $518,608, or 28%. The decrease was primarily attributable to decreases in stock-based compensation expense and a decline in fees paid for professional services, such as corporate legal advice, legal advice for prosecution of patents, auditing and tax services, and other professional services. G&A expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 and 2026, were $6.2 million and $5.1 million, respectively, a decrease of $1.1 million, or 18%. The decrease was primarily attributable to decreases in stock-based compensation expense, professional services and insurance premiums.

 

Legal expenses (recoveries), net: Legal expenses and recoveries, net represent the legal fees that the Company incurred for an Arbitration, net of insurance recoveries. In the nine months ended September 30, 2025, the Company received a $2.0 million insurance recovery, partially offset by $409,055 of legal expenses. In the nine months ended September 30, 2026, there were expenses of $14,483. The Company anticipates it will continue to incur some expenses for the costs of remaining in compliance with the terms of the Settlement and Release Agreement from the Arbitration, primarily due to requirements for patents which are necessary to protect the Company’s exclusive, worldwide intellectual property rights held in perpetuity.

 

Nonoperating changes impacting net income (loss): In the three months ended September 30, 2025, the was a decrease in fair value of $966,284 related to the change in the fair value of the Company’s shares in AlloTera Therapeutics and the related contingent liability, and a loss of $182,146 on the sale of put shares through the Company’s Standby Equity Purchase Agreement. In the three months ended September 30, 2026, there was a $494,683 loss on the return of equipment.

 

In the nine months ended September 30, 2025, there was an increase in fair value of $782,404 related to the change in fair value of the Company’s shares in AlloTera Therapeutics and the related contingent liability, a loss of $182,146 on the sale of put shares through the Company’s Standby Equity Purchase Agreement. In the nine months ended September 30, 2026, there was a $1.8 million nonoperating loss, primarily consisting of a $1.8 million decrease in the fair value of a warrant liability, which was reclassified to equity in this period.

 

Net loss: Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2025 and 2026 was $4.6 million and $3.2 million, respectively. Net loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2025 and 2026, was $8.7 million and $5.0 million, respectively.

About HCW Biologics

 

HCW Biologics Inc. (the “Company”) (NASDAQ: HCWB) is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing transformative fusion immunotherapeutics to treat diseases promoted by chronic inflammation, including autoimmune diseases, cancer, and senescence-associated dysplasia. HCW Biologics is the leader in creation of multi-valent immunotherapeutics with a unique protein-based scaffold, without the antibody Fc domain. The Company’s immunotherapeutics represent a new class of drugs that it believes have the potential to fundamentally change the treatment of proinflammatory and senescence-associated diseases and conditions that are promoted by chronic inflammation —and in doing so, improve patients’ quality of life and possibly extend longevity. A key aspect of the Company’s clinical development and financing strategy is to focus on its business development programs, including its commercial-ready reagents to be used in the production of immunotherapeutics for cancer and infectious diseases. To date, the Company has entered into two licensing agreements in which it has licensed exclusive, worldwide rights for some of its proprietary molecules. See the Company Pipeline at https://hcwbiologics.com/pipeline/
