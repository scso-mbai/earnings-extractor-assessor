NETSOL Technologies reports fiscal first quarter 2027 revenue growth of 9.3% and gross margin expansion to 50.5%

 

First quarter revenue rises 9.3% to $16.4 million; GAAP net income of $0.16 million compared with a net loss in the prior-year period

 

ENCINO, Calif., Nov. 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- NETSOL Technologies, Inc. (Nasdaq: NTWK), a provider of AI-enabled solutions and services powering OEMs, dealerships and financial institutions to sell, finance and lease assets, reported results for its fiscal first quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

First quarter highlights▪

 

▪First quarter revenue increased 9.3% to $16.4 million, compared with $15.0 million in the prior-year period; subscription and support revenue increased 6.1% to $9.5 million.
▪First quarter gross margin expanded by more than 1,100 basis points to 50.5%, compared with 39.4% in the prior-year period; income from operations was $0.4 million, compared with an operating loss of $1.8 million.
▪First quarter GAAP net income attributable to NETSOL was $0.16 million, or $0.01 per diluted share, compared with a net loss of $2.36 million, or $0.21 per diluted share, in the prior-year period.
▪First quarter adjusted EBITDA attributable to NETSOL was $0.48 million, compared with negative $1.66 million in the prior-year period.
▪Net cash used in operating activities was $5.6 million, primarily attributable to working capital; cash and cash equivalents were $19.7 million at September 30, 2026.

 

First quarter fiscal 2027 financial results

 

Revenue

 

Total net revenues for the first quarter of fiscal 2027 increased 9.3% to $16.4 million, compared with $15.0 million in the prior-year period. On a constant currency basis, total net revenues were $16.5 million. First quarter revenue and gross margin were each the highest for a first quarter in the past five fiscal years.

 

Subscription (SaaS and Cloud) and support revenues increased 6.1% to $9.5 million, compared with $9.0 million in the prior-year period.

 

Services revenues increased 6.3% to $6.4 million, compared with $6.0 million in the prior-year period.

 

Gross profit and operating income

 

Gross profit increased to $8.3 million, or 50.5% of net revenues, compared with $5.9 million, or 39.4% of net revenues, in the prior-year period. Gross margin expanded by more than 1,100 basis points.

 

Gross margin benefited from revenue mix and the capitalization of approximately $1.0 million of software development costs, compared with none in the prior-year period.

 

Headcount was 1,272 at September 30, 2026, compared with 1,370 at June 30, 2026 and 1,442 at September 30, 2025.

 

Income from operations was $0.4 million, compared with a loss from operations of $1.8 million in the prior-year period, representing an operating margin of 2.5% compared with negative 12.2%.

 

 
 

 

Net income and non-GAAP results

 

GAAP net income attributable to NETSOL was $0.16 million, or $0.01 per diluted share, compared with a net loss of $2.36 million, or $0.21 per diluted share, in the prior-year period.

 

Consolidated net income was $0.53 million, compared with a consolidated net loss of $2.22 million in the prior-year period. Net income attributable to non-controlling interests was $0.37 million, compared with $0.14 million.

 

The improvement reflects stronger gross margin performance and a significantly narrower foreign currency exchange loss of $7,000, compared with $287,000 in the prior-year period.

 

Non-GAAP results for the first quarter, compared with the prior-year period, were:

 

▪Non-GAAP EBITDA: $0.78 million, compared with negative $1.78 million.
▪Consolidated adjusted EBITDA: $0.89 million, compared with negative $1.35 million.
▪Adjusted EBITDA attributable to NETSOL: $0.48 million, compared with negative $1.66 million.

 

Cash flow and balance sheet

 

Net cash used in operating activities for the first quarter was $5.6 million, compared with $5.3 million provided by operating activities in the prior-year period. The change was primarily attributable to working capital: contract liabilities decreased $3.4 million, contract assets increased $2.6 million and accounts payable and accrued expenses decreased $1.5 million, partially offset by a $1.1 million decrease in accounts receivable. The company also invested approximately $2.0 million in property, equipment and capitalized software development, compared with approximately $0.5 million in the prior-year period. This included approximately $1.0 million of capitalized software development costs, compared with none in the prior-year period, which also contributed to the gross margin improvement.

 

Cash and cash equivalents were $19.7 million at September 30, 2026 compared with $27.1 million at June 30, 2026, reflecting the working capital movements described above. Working capital was $29.1 million at September 30, 2026.

 

NETSOL stockholders’ equity was $42.0 million at September 30, 2026.

 

On October 30, 2026, the board of NETSOL Technologies Limited, the company’s majority-owned subsidiary listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange, recommended a buy-back of up to 4,000,000 of its shares, approximately 4.45% of its issued capital, to be held in treasury and funded from the subsidiary’s distributable profits. The proposal is subject to shareholder approval on December 3, 2026. The company is not assigning a financial benefit to the proposal at this stage.

 

Separately, on October 1, 2026, the Board of NETSOL Technologies Europe Limited began evaluating strategic alternatives for its subsidiary Virtual Lease Services Limited (VLS), including a potential sale or wind-down, though no plan has been approved or agreement executed. VLS revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 represented approximately 1.7% of consolidated revenues, and its total assets represented approximately 1.3% of total consolidated assets. If a sale or wind-down is completed, the company may recognize a loss on disposal and incur exit-related costs, which cannot be estimated at this time.

Revenue, gross margin and profitability all moved in the right direction compared with a year ago, and we did it while continuing to strengthen our commercial pipeline and deepen trust with our largest customers. That combination, growth, margin improvement and deepening customer relationships, is what we mean when we talk about making this company’s performance more predictable.”

 

Asad Ghauri, Global Head of Sales and Group Managing Director of Europe at NETSOL Technologies, Inc., commented:

 

“We saw strong commercial momentum this quarter. We brought Transcend Retail live across two new U.S. dealerships, Atlanta Autos and Royal Coach RV, and BMO Equipment Finance, a 30-year customer, moved from our legacy LeasePak platform onto Transcend Finance, part of a broader pattern we’re seeing across our install base.”

 

Sardar Abubakr, Chief Financial Officer of NETSOL Technologies, Inc., commented:

 

“The first quarter marked a clear improvement from where we stood a year ago. Revenue grew 9.3%, gross margin expanded more than 1,100 basis points and we moved from an operating loss to operating income, with net income attributable to NETSOL of $0.16 million compared with a loss of $2.36 million a year ago.”

 

“In the fourth quarter we billed and collected ahead of delivery, and in the first quarter we delivered against those billings. That is most of the operating cash outflow. Our liquidity is fine and we can fund the business through the year.”

 

“Our priorities for the remainder of the year are unchanged: grow recurring revenue, build on this quarter’s year-over-year margin gains and continue converting consolidated performance into value for NETSOL shareholders.”

About NETSOL Technologies

 

NETSOL Technologies delivers state-of-the-art solutions for asset finance, leasing and digital automotive retail, serving automotive and equipment OEMs, dealerships, auto captives and financial institutions across over 30 countries. Since its inception in 1997, NETSOL has been at the cutting edge of technology, pioneering innovations with its asset finance solutions, and today leverages advanced AI and cloud services to meet the complex needs of the global market. Renowned for its deep industry expertise, customer-centric approach and commitment to excellence, NETSOL fosters strong partnerships with its customers, ensuring their success in an ever-evolving landscape. With a rich history of innovation, ethical business practices and a focus on sustainability, NETSOL is dedicated to empowering businesses worldwide, securing its position as the trusted partner for leading firms around the globe.

Use of Non-GAAP financial measures

 

This press release includes references to non-GAAP EBITDA, non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA, gross, and non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA, net, which are non-GAAP financial measures. Non-GAAP EBITDA is net income before non-controlling interest, income taxes, depreciation and amortization, interest expense and interest income. Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA, gross, further excludes foreign currency exchange gains and losses and non-cash stock-based compensation; non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA, net, further deducts the adjusted EBITDA attributable to non-controlling interests. Management believes these measures assist investors in evaluating operating performance and comparing results across periods. Non-GAAP measures should be considered in addition to, and not as substitutes for, the most directly comparable GAAP measures. Reconciliations of these measures to net income attributable to NETSOL, the most directly comparable GAAP measure, together with an explanation of how management uses these measures, are provided in Schedule 4 of the financial tables that follow.
