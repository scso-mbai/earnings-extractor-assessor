PERFORMANCE SUMMARY (Reported)
MINNEAPOLIS (October 27, 2026) - Polaris Inc. (NYSE: PII) (the "Company") today released third quarter 2026 results. For the third quarter, the Company reported worldwide sales of $2,043 million, up 11 percent versus the third quarter of 2025. North America sales of $1,815 million represented 89 percent of total Company sales and increased 14 percent from $1,586 million in the third quarter of 2025. International sales of $228 million represented 11 percent of total Company sales and decreased 11 percent versus the third quarter of 2025. Total Company sales in the third quarter of 2026 were positively impacted by higher shipment volumes and positive net price partially offset by the divestiture of Indian Motorcycle.

Gross profit margin increased 52 basis points to 21.2 percent for the third quarter, as compared to the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted gross profit margin of 21.2 percent increased 53 basis points primarily driven by positive net price and operational efficiencies somewhat offset by higher commodity costs compared to the third quarter of 2025.

Operating expenses were $376 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $380 million in the third quarter of 2025. Operating expenses as a percentage of sales were 18.4 percent, down 223 basis points in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025.

For the third quarter, net income attributable to Polaris was $38 million, or $0.66 per diluted share, compared to a net loss attributable to Polaris of $16 million, or $0.28 net loss per diluted share in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income attributable to Polaris for the quarter was $63 million and adjusted EPS was $1.08.

Polaris Powersports segment results were primarily driven by these factors:

2026 BUSINESS OUTLOOK
The Company is raising 2026 full year adjusted sales and adjusted EPS guidance. The Company now expects 2026 adjusted sales to be between $7.55 billion to $7.60 billion versus prior guidance of $7.30 billion to $7.50 billion. The Company now expects adjusted EPS of $3.50 to $3.60 versus prior guidance of $3.00 to $3.10, of which approximately $1.09 is attributed to the benefit of tariff refunds recorded in the second and third quarters of 2026.

ABOUT POLARIS
As the global leader in powersports, Polaris Inc. (NYSE: PII) has been defining and redefining outdoor adventure since 1954. Polaris delivers industry-shaping off-road vehicles, snowmobiles, boats, military, quadricycles, and commercial transportation vehicles, along with an expansive portfolio of parts, garments, and accessories. Its lineup includes some of the most iconic brands in powersports including the RANGER, RZR, Polaris XPEDITION, Bennington pontoons, Slingshot, and more. Headquartered in Minnesota and serving customers in nearly 100 countries, Polaris continues to set the standard for performance, quality, and unmatched service. Explore more at www.polaris.com.

NON-GAAP ADJUSTMENTS
Third Quarter 2026 Results

Restructuring Costs
The Company is executing certain corporate restructuring activities across the organization to increase efficiency and focus its business. For the third quarter of 2026, Polaris recorded costs totaling $1.6 million, which was included as a non-GAAP adjustment.

Intangible Amortization Related to Acquisitions
The Company uses an adjusted net income (loss) metric which excludes intangible amortization from all historical business acquisitions. The Company believes this non-GAAP information is useful to understanding its operating results and the ongoing performance of its underlying businesses because the amount and timing of such charges are significantly impacted by the timing, size, number and nature of the acquisitions the Company completes. For the third quarter of 2026, Polaris recorded $4.4 million of intangible amortization related to acquisitions as a non-GAAP adjustment.

Loss on Disposal Groups
For the third quarter of 2026, Polaris recorded net adjustments of $1.8 million related to the separation of its Indian Motorcycle business and impairment and other charges related to certain other assets sold. These adjustments were included as non-GAAP adjustments.

Additional Adjustments
For the third quarter of 2026, the Company recorded $24.6 million of expenses related to pending class action lawsuit settlements and the related legal costs, as well as certain costs associated with the Company's active patent infringement matter. These expenses were included as a non-GAAP adjustment.
