DOUGLAS ELLIMAN INC. REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS
Third Quarter Results with Operating Income of $6.3M; Net Income of $7.4M

Cash, cash equivalents and a U.S. Treasury Bill of approximately $123 million, with no long-term debt as of September 30, 2026, and a Development Marketing Pipeline of $26.0 billion

Announces $10 Million Share Repurchase Program


MIAMI, FL, November 9, 2026 - Douglas Elliman Inc. (“Douglas Elliman” or the “Company”) (NYSE: DOUG), the parent company of Douglas Elliman Realty, one of the nation's premier luxury residential real estate brokerages, today announced financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

CEO STATEMENT

“Over the past two years, we have worked to build the foundation for Douglas Elliman’s future, and we have succeeded in creating a stronger, more focused company,” said Michael S. Liebowitz, President and Chief Executive Officer of Douglas Elliman Inc. “In the third quarter, we delivered operating income of $6.3 million and net income of $7.4 million, while maintaining approximately $123 million in cash, cash equivalents, and a U.S. Treasury Bill and no long-term debt. We have sharpened our focus around what we do best, and we are now investing in our people and technology, modernizing our operating platform and pursuing opportunities that reinforce Douglas Elliman’s leadership in luxury residential real estate and drive our next phase of growth. The Board’s approval of a $10 million share repurchase program further reflects our confidence in Douglas Elliman and our commitment to delivering long-term value for our stockholders.”

Q3 2026 FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS
Three months ended September 30, 2026
Third quarter 2026 revenues were $252.1 million, compared to revenues of $262.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The Company disposed of its property management business in October 2025 and, excluding property management revenues, revenues were $253.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. The Company recorded an operating income of $6.3 million in the


third quarter of 2026, compared to an operating loss of $10.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. Net income attributed to Douglas Elliman Inc. in the third quarter of 2026 was $7.4 million, or $0.08 per diluted common share, compared to a net loss of $24.7 million, or $0.29 per diluted common share, in the third quarter of 2025.
Nine months ended September 30, 2026
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, revenues were $749.9 million, compared to revenues of $787.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Excluding property management revenues, revenues were $758.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The Company recorded an operating loss of $14.6 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to an operating loss of $21.5 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. Net loss attributed to Douglas Elliman Inc. for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $11.6 million, or $0.14 per diluted common share, compared to $53.3 million, or $0.63 per diluted common share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

Reconciliations of non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 are included in Tables 2 and 3, and for the last twelve months (“LTM”) ended September 30, 2026 and year ended December 31, 2025 are included in Table 2.
Three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025

GROSS TRANSACTION VALUE
For the third quarter of 2026, the Company achieved gross transaction value of approximately $10.0 billion with an average price per transaction of $1.83 million. For the third quarter of 2025, the Company achieved gross transaction value of approximately $10.0 billion with an average price per transaction of $1.77 million.
For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company achieved gross transaction value of approximately $29.4 billion with an average price per transaction of $1.88 million. For the nine months ended September 30, 2025, the Company achieved gross transaction value of approximately $30.1 billion with an average price per transaction of $1.87 million.
BALANCE SHEET AND CAPITAL POSITION
Douglas Elliman maintained a robust balance sheet as of September 30, 2026, with cash and cash equivalents of approximately $102.7 million as well as an additional $20 million in a U.S. Treasury Bill and no long-term debt. This financial profile provides the Company with flexibility to return capital to stockholders, invest across its strategic priorities, including people, technology and operations, and pursue disciplined growth opportunities.

The Board of Directors has approved a share repurchase program authorizing the Company to repurchase up to $10 million of its common stock, reflecting the Company’s continued focus on disciplined capital allocation and long-term stockholder value. The authorization does not obligate the Company to repurchase any particular amount of common stock during any period and the program may be modified or suspended at any time at the Company's discretion. Stock repurchases may be made from time to time and the actual amount repurchased will depend on a variety of factors including market conditions, cash flow, and liquidity needs, regulatory and legal requirements, and other factors. The stock repurchases may be made in both open market and privately negotiated transactions and may include the use of derivative contracts, structured share repurchase agreements, and Rule 10b5-1 and Rule 10b-18 trading plans. Repurchases are expected to be funded from cash on hand. The Company had approximately 91 million shares of common stock outstanding as of November 4, 2026.


OUTLOOK

Looking ahead, the Company has a development marketing pipeline of approximately $26 billion, with an additional $11.8 billion scheduled to come to market through December 31, 2027. The Company is also advancing its AI transformation, which is expected to gradually drive significant savings in non-commission operating expenses over the next three years.

Douglas Elliman remains focused on positioning the Company to deliver long-term stockholder value by modernizing its operating platform, strengthening operational efficiency, investing in exceptional agent talent, and pursuing disciplined growth in key luxury markets.

STRATEGIC GROWTH INITIATIVES

Douglas Elliman continues to focus on its luxury residential real estate business while investing in its people, technology, operations and related capabilities.
Technology and AI Investment

Douglas Elliman continues to advance its company-wide technology transformation to support its evolution into a technology-forward real estate brokerage. The Company is working to modernize workflows, processes and infrastructure across the organization, with a focus on improving efficiency and providing agents and employees with better tools to support their work.
The Company remains focused on gradually realizing efficiencies in non-commission operating expenses beginning in 2027.

Recently, Douglas Elliman added dedicated leadership to support these efforts. Sharon Milz joined Douglas Elliman Realty as Head of AI Technology Transformation, overseeing the Company’s enterprise AI strategy and deployment. Nick Patino joined Douglas Elliman Realty as Director of AI Enablement, focused on integrating AI capabilities into the Company’s operations and driving adoption and productivity.

Operational Leadership

Douglas Elliman Realty also appointed Jamie Gagliano as Chief Operations Officer, Brokerage. In this role, she is focused on the Company’s national brokerage operations, including opportunities to simplify processes, strengthen support for agents and build a more scalable organization.
