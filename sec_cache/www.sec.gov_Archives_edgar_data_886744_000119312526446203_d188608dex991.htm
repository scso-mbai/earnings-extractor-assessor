Geron Corporation Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Recent Business Highlights

Achieved $60.1 million in RYTELO® (imetelstat) net product revenue in Q3 2026, an increase of 5% compared to Q2 2026

Reiterated 2026 RYTELO net product revenue at the mid-to-high end of the $220 million to $240 million range; lowered 2026 total operating expenses range to $220 million to $230 million

Initiated gated commercial launch to expand RYTELO access to eligible LR-MDS patients in targeted European markets, with availability expected in Germany by end of year

Ended Q3 2026 with cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash, and marketable securities of $339 million

Company to host conference call and webcast today, November 4, 2026, at 8:00 a.m. ET

FOSTER CITY, Calif., November 4, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – Geron Corporation (Nasdaq: GERN), a hematology-focused commercial stage biopharmaceutical company pursuing more meaningful moments for people living with blood cancer, today reported financial results for the third quarter of 2026 and recent business highlights.

“Another quarter of strong RYTELO demand growth reinforces our confidence in the LR-MDS commercial opportunity and our ability to reach more second-line patients. Increased demand growth is being driven by the community practices which, in our view, indicates the potential for RYTELO to have a long growth runway in the U.S.,” said Harout Semerjian, President and Chief Executive Officer of Geron. “Outside the U.S. there is a clear opportunity for us to serve more patients and create additional value by broadening RYTELO’s access. As such, we have initiated a gated commercial launch to bring RYTELO to targeted EU markets with availability in Germany expected by the end of the year. Expanding access to therapies, along with pursing opportunistic innovation, is a key part of building a leading hematology company.”

Recent Business Highlights

 

  •  

Reported RYTELO net product revenue of $60.1 million in the third quarter of 2026.

 

  •  

Grew RYTELO demand by 10% in the third quarter 2026, compared to the second quarter 2026.

 

  •  

Increased ordering accounts by roughly 8% in the third quarter 2026 to approximately 1,700.

Initiated a gated commercial launch to bring RYTELO to eligible LR-MDS patients in targeted European markets. RYTELO is expected to be available in Germany later this year and France in 2027.

Exhibited three posters at the Society of Hematologic Oncology (SOHO) 2026 Annual Meeting, including real-world evidence of imetelstat for the treatment of LR-MDS that was generally consistent with the Phase 3 IMerge trial and an updated post-hoc analysis of the Phase 2 IMbark study of imetelstat for the treatment of relapsed/refractory myelofibrosis.1-3

Nominated for the 2026 Prix Galien USA Award for Best Product for Rare/Orphan Diseases for RYTELO in LR-MDS, an award recognizing extraordinary scientific achievements in transforming health and improving lives.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Cash and Marketable Securities

As of September 30, 2026, Geron had approximately $339.4 million in cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash and marketable securities, compared to $326.9 million as of June 30, 2026, which provides the Company with cash to fund its operating requirements for the foreseeable future.

Net Income (Loss)

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company reported a net income of $1.4 million, or $0.00 per share, compared to a net loss of $18.4 million, or $0.03 per share, for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The change in net income (loss) is primarily attributable to increased revenue and continued financial discipline lowering operating expenses across the company.

Revenues

Total product revenue, net for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $60.1 million, compared to $47.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

Costs and Operating Expenses

Total costs and operating expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were $54.4 million, compared to $61.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease is primarily due to lower personnel-related and clinical development expenses, partially offset by non-cash inventory-related expenses.

Cost of goods sold was approximately $6.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $1.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, which consisted of costs to manufacture and distribute RYTELO. The increase is primarily due to non-cash inventory-related expenses.

Research and development expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were $12.7 million, compared to $21.1 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease in research and development expenses was primarily a result of lower personnel-related expenses and lower clinical and chemistry, manufacturing, and controls (CMC) costs.

Selling, general and administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026, were $35.7 million, compared to $39.0 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily due to lower personnel-related expenses.

2026 Financial Guidance

For fiscal year 2026, the Company continues to expect RYTELO net product revenue to be at the mid-to-high end of the $220 million to $240 million range. Geron now expects total operating expenses to be between $220 million and $230 million, down from its prior guidance range of $230 million to $240 million. Total operating expenses include non-cash items such as stock-based compensation expense, amortization of debt discounts and issuance costs, depreciation, and amortization.

Based on current operating plans and assumptions, the Company believes that its existing cash, cash equivalents, restricted cash and marketable securities, together with anticipated net revenues from U.S. sales of RYTELO, will be sufficient to fund projected operating requirements for the foreseeable future.

About RYTELO (imetelstat)

RYTELO (imetelstat) is an oligonucleotide telomerase inhibitor approved in the U.S. for the treatment of adult patients with lower-risk myelodysplastic syndromes (LR-MDS) with transfusion-dependent anemia requiring four or more red blood cell units over eight weeks who have not responded to or have lost response to or are ineligible for erythropoiesis-stimulating agents (ESAs). It is indicated to be administered as an intravenous infusion over two hours every four weeks.

In addition, RYTELO is approved in the European Union as a monotherapy for the treatment of adult patients with transfusion-dependent anemia due to very low, low or intermediate risk myelodysplastic syndromes without an isolated deletion 5q cytogenetic (non-del 5q) abnormality and who had an unsatisfactory response to or are ineligible for erythropoietin-based therapy.

RYTELO is a first-in-class treatment that works by inhibiting telomerase enzymatic activity. Telomeres are protective caps at the end of chromosomes that naturally shorten each time a cell divides. In LR-MDS, abnormal bone marrow cells often express the enzyme telomerase, which rebuilds those telomeres, allowing for uncontrolled cell division. Developed and exclusively owned by Geron, RYTELO is the first and only telomerase inhibitor approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration and the European Commission.

Please see RYTELO (imetelstat) full Prescribing Information, including Medication Guide, available at https://pi.geron.com/products/US/pi/rytelo_pi.pdf.
