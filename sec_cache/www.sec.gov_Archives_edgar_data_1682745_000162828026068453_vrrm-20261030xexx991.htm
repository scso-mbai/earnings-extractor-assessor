Verra Mobility Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Total revenue of $258.9 million
•Net income of $40.4 million
•Net cash provided from operations of $114.5 million
•Record bookings quarter in Government Solutions, excluding quarter in which we booked the New York City Department of Transportation (“NYCDOT”) contract renewal
•Reaffirming fiscal year 2026 guidance provided August 5, 2026
•Board appointed Matthew Kvarda as Interim Chief Financial Officer
•Update on Commercial Services’ customer contracts
MESA, Ariz., October 30, 2026 /PRNewswire/ – Verra Mobility Corporation (NASDAQ: VRRM), a leading provider of smart mobility technology solutions, announced today the financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights
•Revenue: Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $258.9 million, a decrease of 1% compared to $261.9 million for the third quarter of 2025. Service revenue decreased 1%, driven by a 14% decline in our Commercial Services segment partially offset by 13% growth in our Government Solutions segment.
•Net income and Diluted Earnings Per Share (“EPS”): Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $40.4 million, or $0.27 per share, based on 152.1 million diluted weighted average shares outstanding. Net income for the comparable 2025 period was $46.8 million, or $0.29 per share, based on 161.9 million diluted weighted average shares outstanding. The decrease in net income for the third quarter of 2026 was primarily due to decreased revenue and margins.
•Adjusted EPS*: Adjusted EPS for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.36 per share compared to $0.37 per share for the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted EBITDA*: Adjusted EBITDA was $103.9 million for the third quarter of 2026 compared to $113.3 million for the same period in 2025. Adjusted EBITDA Margin* was 40% and 43% of total revenue for the 2026 and 2025 periods, respectively. The primary driver of the Adjusted EBITDA margin decline was an approximate 560 basis point decline in the Commercial Services segment profit margin.
•Net Cash Provided from Operations: Cash provided by operating activities increased by $36.8 million from $77.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025 to $114.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Net income quarter-over-quarter decreased by $6.4 million, from $46.8 million in 2025 to $40.4 million in 2026. The aggregate adjustments to reconcile net income to net cash provided by operating activities decreased $12.3 million mainly due to decreases in stock-based compensation, deferred income taxes, and an uncertain tax position reserve release. The aggregate changes in operating assets and liabilities increased by $55.5 million in 2026 compared to the prior year period, of which the majority was attributable to a decrease in accounts receivable, partially offset by a decrease in accounts payable.

We report our results of operations based on three operating segments:
•Commercial Services offers automated toll and violations management and title and registration solutions to rental car companies, fleet management companies and other large fleet owners.
•Government Solutions delivers automated safety solutions to municipalities, school districts and government agencies, including services and technology that enable photo enforcement cameras to detect and process traffic violations related to speed, red-light, school bus and city bus lane management.
•Parking Solutions provides an integrated suite of parking software, transaction processing and hardware solutions to universities, municipalities, parking operators, healthcare facilities and transportation hubs in the United States and Canada.
Third Quarter 2026 Segment Detail
•The Commercial Services segment generated total revenue of $100.3 million, a 14% decrease compared to $117.3 million in the same period in 2025. Segment profit was $61.3 million, a 22% decrease from $78.3 million in the prior year period. The decreases in revenue and segment profit compared to the prior year period resulted from a $16.1 million or 20% reduction in rental car companies (“RAC”) tolling revenue, due to the effect of contract extensions entered into with two of our significant customers on terms materially less favorable to us than those of the prior agreement, fleet volume fluctuations with one significant customer, and reduced travel demand compared to the same period in 2025. U.S Transportation and Security Administration passenger volume in the third quarter of 2026 was approximately 3% less compared to the prior year period. Revenue derived from fleet management companies decreased $2.2 million or 13% in 2026 compared to the same period in 2025 due to a one-time operational adjustment and prior period churn. Normalizing for these adjustments, fleet management revenue would have grown 4% over the prior year period. The segment profit margin was 61% for the third quarter of 2026 and 67% for the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 segment profit margins declined due to reduced RAC tolling revenue, increased credit loss expense and decreased volume rebates earned from total spend on credit card transactions due to decreased tolling and travel activity.
•The Government Solutions segment generated total revenue of $136.8 million, a 12% increase compared to $122.6 million in the same period in 2025. The increase was due to a 13% increase in service revenue over the prior year period, primarily driven by an increase of $8.6 million attributable to expansion in speed, red light, bus lane, school bus and other services, outside of NYCDOT. The remaining $5.6 million of growth, or 12%, is attributable to NYCDOT installation revenue on new camera installations, net of pricing changes under the new contract. In addition, product revenue increased approximately $0.1 million from the prior year period. The segment profit was $39.1 million in 2026 compared to $31.3 million in the prior year period with segment profit margins of 29% for 2026 and 26% for 2025. The increase in segment profit margins compared to the prior year period was primarily driven by NYCDOT installation volume, revenue mix and decreased wage expenses.
•The Parking Solutions segment generated total revenue of $21.7 million, a 2% decrease compared to $22.1 million in the same period in 2025, which was due primarily to a decrease in professional services and subscription services related to parking management solutions, partially offset by increases in software as a service (“SaaS”) product offerings compared to the prior year period. Change in Executive Leadership and Organizational Realignment
As previously announced, on September 30, 2026, the Board appointed Jon Newhard as President and Chief Executive Officer of Verra Mobility, effective November 1, 2026, following a comprehensive search led by the Board and an executive search firm. He will succeed Jon Keyser, who has served as Interim President and Chief Executive Officer during the leadership transition. Mr. Newhard brings more than two decades of experience in the transportation and mobility industry. His leadership will support the continued execution of Verra Mobility’s strategy, build on the Company’s strong foundation and position the business for long-term growth. To help facilitate a smooth transition, Mr. Keyser has agreed to serve as Special Advisor through the remainder of 2026.
On October 15, 2026, we announced that Craig Conti will step down as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, effective November 4, 2026. The Board has retained a leading executive search firm, Spencer Stuart, to conduct a comprehensive search for a permanent CFO who can help advance the Company’s strategy and support its next phase of growth.
On October 29, 2026, the Board appointed Matthew Kvarda as Interim Chief Financial Officer, effective November 5, 2026, to serve until a permanent Chief Financial Officer is appointed.
Mr. Kvarda is a Managing Director of Alvarez & Marsal North America, LLC (“A&M”) in Los Angeles, California where he has worked since 2004. At A&M, Mr. Kvarda has served as Interim Chief Financial Officer or Interim senior management for a variety of public and non-public companies including Jacuzzi Brands, TEAM, Inc., Revlon, Inc. and 23andMe. Prior to joining A&M, Mr. Kvarda was a Senior Director at KPMG, LLP from 2002 to 2003. Mr. Kvarda was also a Senior Director with Arthur Andersen & Co. from 1996 to 2002 and also served as an Assistant Vice President at Bank of America from 1992 to 1996. Mr. Kvarda earned a bachelor’s degree in economics from the University of California, San Diego , and an MBA from the University of California, Los Angeles. The Board believes that Mr. Kvarda’s extensive financial experience and prior experience serving as Interim Chief Financial Officer make him well suited to serve as Interim Chief Financial Officer of the Company.
Following the pending executive leadership transition, and upon evaluating how the company is organized and managed, the company will make a determination as to its future segment reporting.
Commercial Services Customer Contracts
In July 2026, we entered into a seven-year contract extension with Avis Budget Group ("ABG") following the withdrawal of its previously issued termination notice. The revised agreement included materially less favorable terms compared to the prior contract and provided ABG with greater flexibility to adjust its fleet volumes.
During the third quarter, ABG significantly and abruptly reduced its fleet volumes under the parties’ agreement, contrary to its prior stated intent to continue the parties’ longstanding relationship. Given the significant reduction in fleet volumes and other factors, we determined that our mutual interests were no longer aligned, and terminating the contract was in Verra Mobility’s best interest. As a result, on October 2, 2026, Verra Mobility exercised its contractual right to terminate the agreement effective on January 30, 2027. We have engaged Quinn Emanuel Urquhart & Sullivan, LLP to vigorously support our protection of intellectual property, trade secrets and confidential information related to our tolling and violations solutions. About Verra Mobility
Verra Mobility Corporation (NASDAQ: VRRM) is a leading provider of smart mobility technology solutions that make transportation safer, smarter, and more connected. The company sits at the center of the mobility ecosystem, bringing together vehicles, hardware, software, data, and people to enable safe, efficient solutions for customers globally. Verra Mobility’s transportation safety systems and parking management solutions protect lives, improve urban and motorway mobility, and support healthier communities. The company also solves complex payment, utilization, and compliance challenges for fleet owners and rental car companies. Headquartered in Arizona, Verra Mobility operates in the United States, Australia, Europe, and Canada. For more information, please visit www.verramobility.com.

EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA
We define “EBITDA” as net income adjusted to exclude interest expense, net, income taxes, depreciation and amortization. “Adjusted EBITDA” further excludes certain non-cash expenses and non-recurring items.
Free Cash Flow
We define “Free Cash Flow” as net cash flow provided by operating activities less purchases of installation and service parts and property and equipment.
Adjusted Net Income
We define “Adjusted Net Income” as net income adjusted to exclude amortization of intangibles and certain non-cash or non-recurring expenses such as loss on extinguishment of debt, among other items.
Adjusted EPS
We define “Adjusted EPS” as Adjusted Net Income divided by the diluted weighted average shares for the period.

Adjusted EBITDA Margin
We define “Adjusted EBITDA Margin” as Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of total revenue.
Net Debt
We define “Net Debt” as total debt, net excluding original issue discounts and unamortized deferred financing costs, less cash and cash equivalents.
Net Leverage
We define “Net Leverage” as Net Debt divided by the trailing twelve months Adjusted EBITDA as of the current quarter-end.
Additional Metrics
Recurring Revenue or Recurring Service Revenue
