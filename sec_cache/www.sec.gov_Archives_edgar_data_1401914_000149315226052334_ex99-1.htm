Daré Bioscience Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Highlights Commercial Execution and Pipeline Advancement

 

DARE to PLAY™ Sildenafil Cream prescription dispensing activities underway this month, with prescriptions written across 48 states and Washington, D.C.

 

Commercial launch of Flora Sync LF5™ showing encouraging early indicators, including orders from 27 states, provider office reorder and positive consumer feedback

 

Company surpassed $43 million in cumulative non-dilutive grant funding supporting advancement of DARE-LARC1; exploring potential strategic collaboration opportunities in areas including obesity, diabetes, hormone therapies and neurologic disease, including potential applications with GLP-1 medicines, for the underlying DARE-IDDS platform

 

Company to host conference call and webcast today, November 16, 2026, at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Time – Access the webcast here

 

SAN DIEGO, November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Daré Bioscience, Inc. (NASDAQ: DARE), a purpose-driven health biotech company solely focused on closing the gap in women’s health between promising science and real-world solutions, today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provided a corporate update highlighting commercial execution and continued progress with its development pipeline.

 

“This is an exciting day for clinicians and women waiting for DARE to PLAY Sildenafil Cream, as the first shipment is on route from the outsourcing facility to Medvantx, the central pharmacy that handles order processing and dispensing. DARE to PLAY provider engagement built demand ahead of dispensing, with prescriptions written across 48 states and Washington, D.C. Our outsourcing facility partner has been working to manufacture supply at a scale to meet anticipated demand, and, with product now on route to Medvantx, we expect prescription dispensing to commence later this month,” said Sabrina Martucci Johnson, Founder, President and Chief Executive Officer of Daré Bioscience.

 

 

 

 

 

“For Flora Sync LF5 we built our initial commercial approach around the relationships women have with their healthcare providers, given the clinical data that distinguish Flora Sync LF5 in the category, and the early response to Flora Sync LF5 is encouraging. Recommendations and sampling are helping women discover the product, while our provider office reorder gives us an early indication of repeat demand,” continued Ms. Johnson. “Our priority now is to build on that response and turn initial purchases into sustained growth. We look forward to launching Flora Sync LF5 on Amazon in early 2027, and are pleased to be expanding the DARE to RESTORE™ brand family to include Flora Renew™ and Flora Reset™, a vulvar mask and cleanser, also anticipated in early 2027.”

 

BUILDING TOWARD A MULTI-PRODUCT COMMERCIAL PORTFOLIO

 

Daré Bioscience is building a commercial platform designed to make it easier for women to discover, access and purchase products developed specifically for their health needs. The Company’s approach is to bring together evidence-based products, healthcare provider engagement and sampling, targeted digital marketing, telehealth services and the DARE Health Hub to support adoption and repeat purchases across a growing commercial portfolio.

 

Key Commercial Highlights:

 

  ● Flora Sync LF5™: Launched in June 2026, with first sales in July, Flora Sync LF5 is Daré Bioscience’s first directly commercialized product and initial consumer health offering. Available through the DARE Health Hub and directly to healthcare providers for in-office dispensing, the product has generated orders from 27 states and its first provider office reorder. Early consumer feedback includes a 4.9-star review rating after the initial approximately 25 reviews, with customers reporting purchases following a provider recommendation or sample. The Company continues clinician education, sampling and provider outreach to build recommendations, repeat office purchases and consumer demand.
     
  ● DARE to PLAY™ Sildenafil Cream*: Daré Bioscience is preparing for prescription dispensing of its proprietary topical sildenafil cream through the Section 503B compounding pathway, with dispensing expected this month in 28 states. The Company has worked with its 503B-registered outsourcing facility partner to prepare to supply at a scale to support anticipated demand, as well as to continue efforts to obtain licenses to dispense DARE to PLAY Sildenafil Cream in the remaining states. Provider engagement has generated prescriptions from 182 office-based prescribers, excluding telehealth. Across office-based providers and telehealth, prescriptions have been written in 48 states and Washington, D.C.

 

 

 

 

 

  ● DARE to RECLAIM™*: Daré Bioscience is preparing for commercial availability of its monthly estradiol and progesterone intravaginal ring through the Section 503B compounding pathway, with prescription dispensing targeted to begin in 2027. In parallel, the Company plans to continue conventional drug product development necessary to pursue a regulatory strategy using the FDA’s 505(b)(2) pathway.
     
  ● DARE Health Hub: The Company’s digital commercial platform continues to support product discovery, education, access and fulfillment, providing infrastructure intended to support current and future product offerings.

 

ADVANCING A DIFFERENTIATED WOMEN’S HEALTH PIPELINE

 

Daré Bioscience continues to progress a diversified pipeline to address significant unmet needs across women’s health, supported in part by non-dilutive grant funding.

 

“Non-dilutive funding is central to how we develop our pipeline. We have secured more than $75 million in grant awards to provide funding support for our programs that does not dilute our shareholders, and we continue to pursue additional funding opportunities,” added Ms. Johnson. “That investment is also creating opportunities beyond the programs it initially supported. DARE-IDDS, our programmable drug delivery technology underlying our DARE-LARC1 contraceptive program, is designed to control how much drug is released and when. This tunability has potential applications in obesity, diabetes, hormone therapies and neurological diseases. We are exploring potential strategic collaborations for the platform into these areas, with the goal of reducing the burden of frequent injections or daily dosing.”

 

  ● Ovaprene® – Investigational Hormone-Free Monthly Intravaginal Contraceptive: Daré Bioscience previously reported positive interim results from its ongoing pivotal Phase 3 study of Ovaprene (ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT06127199), and the independent Data Safety Monitoring Board’s recommendation that the study continue without modification. The Company continues to anticipate reporting topline data from the study in 2027. The program remains a key component of Daré Bioscience’s clinical pipeline and is supported by grant funding from the Gates Foundation.
     
  ● DARE-HPV – Investigational Therapeutic for Persistent High-Risk HPV Infection: Daré Bioscience is advancing its Phase 2 clinical study of DARE-HPV, an investigational fixed-dose formulation of lopinavir and ritonavir in a soft gel vaginal insert, to evaluate the safety and antiviral activity of DARE-HPV in women with persistent high-risk human papillomavirus (HPV) infection (ClinicalTrials.gov ID: NCT07601074). The study is expected to enroll approximately 100 women, with topline results anticipated in 2027. The program is supported by funding from the Advanced Research Projects Agency for Health (ARPA-H) and the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases (NIAID) of the National Institutes of Health. There are currently no FDA-approved pharmacologic treatments for HPV infection.

 

 

 

 

 

  ● DARE-PTB2 – Investigational Program for the Prevention of Preterm Birth: Daré Bioscience continues to advance DARE-PTB2, an early-stage program focused on a novel therapeutic approach intended to address biological mechanisms associated with preterm birth, supported by an approximately $0.9 million grant award from the Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute of Child Health and Human Development (NICHD) of the National Institutes of Health announced in August 2026.
     
  ● DARE-LARC1 – Investigational Long-Acting Reversible Contraceptive: Daré Bioscience continues to advance preclinical development of DARE-LARC1, a grant-supported contraceptive program built on the Company’s programmable drug delivery technology. The underlying DARE-IDDS platform is designed to control the amount of drug released and the timing of release, offering potential flexibility in how medicines are delivered.
     
  ● DARE-IDDS – Investigational Programmable Drug Delivery Platform: DARE-IDDS is the underlying technology behind DARE-LARC1 and is designed to control how much drug is released and when. Daré Bioscience believes this tunability may have broader applications across chronic conditions and is exploring potential strategic collaboration opportunities in areas including obesity, diabetes, hormone therapies and neurologic disease, including potential applications with GLP-1 medicines.

 

NON-DILUTIVE GRANT FUNDING

 

Daré Bioscience has secured more than $75 million in non-dilutive grant awards as part of its strategy to fund development across its women’s health pipeline while limiting shareholder dilution. The Company continues to pursue additional grant funding to support its programs and expand development opportunities.

 

Recent Grant Awards:

 

  ● Ovaprene and Contraceptive Product Candidate: In November 2026, Daré Bioscience received a payment of approximately $0.9 million under its November 2024 grant agreement with the Gates Foundation supporting the ongoing Phase 3 clinical trial of Ovaprene and the identification and development of a novel non-hormonal intravaginal contraceptive candidate. This payment represents the next-to-last anticipated tranche under the up to approximately $10.7 million grant award. Daré Bioscience received approximately $5.4 million in funding under the agreement in 2024 and approximately $3.6 million in 2025.
     
  ● DARE-LARC1: In October 2026, Daré Bioscience received a $1.5 million payment under its June 2021 grant agreement supporting development of DARE-LARC1. Including this payment, the Company has received approximately $43.3 million of the up to $49.0 million in potential funding under the agreement.

 

 

 

 

 

FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR THIRD QUARTER 2026 AND HIGHLIGHTS

 

Cash Position

 

As of September 30, 2026, Daré Bioscience had approximately $11.5 million in cash and cash equivalents and working capital of approximately $3.2 million.

 

Revenue

 

Total revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $48,000, compared to approximately $2,300 for the same period of the prior year. The increase was primarily attributable to research and development services revenue under agreements entered into with the Gates Foundation in September and October 2025. Third quarter 2026 revenue includes revenue from sales of Flora Sync LF5, which began in July 2026.

 

Cost of Revenues

 

Cost of revenues for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $0.2 million, with no comparable activity in the same period of the prior year. Cost of revenues primarily related to expenses associated with medical education and awareness related to the commercialization of DARE to PLAY.

 

Operating Expenses

 

Selling, general and administrative expenses were approximately $2.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to approximately $2.5 million for the same period of the prior year. The increase of approximately $0.1 million was primarily attributable to increased commercial-readiness expenses associated with execution of the Company’s expanded business strategy, increased stock-based compensation and personnel costs, and a non-cash write-down of previously deferred offering costs, partially offset by decreases in general corporate overhead expenses and professional services.

 

 

 

 

 

Research and development (R&D) expenses were approximately $0.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to approximately $1.2 million for the same period of the prior year. The decrease was primarily attributable to decreased costs for several development programs, including Sildenafil Cream and DARE to PLAY Sildenafil Cream, DARE-LARC1, and Ovaprene. These decreases were partially offset by increased expenses related to other advanced clinical-stage programs, primarily DARE-HPV, and decreased contra-R&D expenses associated with direct program costs for grant-supported programs.

 

Daré Bioscience recognizes amounts received under grant and governmental funding awards as “contra-R&D expenses,” meaning grant funding reduces R&D expenses in the statements of operations as the related costs are incurred over the grant period. The Company believes this is important to understand when evaluating the scale of the Company’s R&D investment and capital efficiency. Total contra-R&D expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 were approximately $4.7 million and $5.0 million, respectively.

 

Net Loss

 

Net loss for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $3.2 million, compared to approximately $3.6 million for the same period of the prior year.

 

Revenue Outlook

 

Daré Bioscience began recording revenue from sales of Flora Sync LF5 in July 2026 and expects to begin recording revenue from sales of DARE to PLAY Sildenafil Cream in the fourth quarter of 2026. The Company does not expect the amount of such product revenue to be material during 2026. Revenue from DARE to RECLAIM estradiol and progesterone intravaginal ring is targeted to begin in 2027. The Company is building toward a multi-product revenue profile that diversifies and grows across 2026 and 2027.

ABOUT DARÉ BIOSCIENCE, INC.

 

Daré Bioscience (NASDAQ: DARE) is a purpose-driven health biotech company solely focused on closing the gap in women’s health between promising science and real-world solutions. Every innovation Daré Bioscience advances is based in advanced science and backed by rigorous, peer-reviewed research. From contraception to menopause, sexual health to fertility, vaginal health to infectious disease, Daré Bioscience is working to close critical gaps in care using science that serves her needs. *The terms DARE to PLAY and DARE to RECLAIM describe 503B compounded drug products. Compounded drug products are not approved by the FDA. The FDA does not evaluate compounded drug products for safety, effectiveness, or quality. References to Section 503B, 503B, 503B compounding, 503B compounded product, and similar terms refer to Section 503B of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act (FDCA) and the production and supply of compounded drugs by Section 503B-registered outsourcing facilities without patient-specific prescriptions in accordance with Section 503B. Section 503B-registered outsourcing facilities are subject to FDA inspection and required to compound drug products under current Good Manufacturing Practice (cGMP) regulations to ensure quality, strength and consistency.

 

**The term FDA 505(b)(2) regulatory pathway refers to Section 505(b)(2) of the FDCA, which enables an applicant to rely, in part, on the FDA’s prior findings of safety and efficacy data for an existing product, or published literature, in support of the applicant’s new drug application (NDA), providing an alternate path to FDA approval for new or improved formulations or new uses of previously approved drugs.
