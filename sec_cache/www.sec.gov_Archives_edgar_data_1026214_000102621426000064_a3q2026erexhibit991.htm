McLean, VA — Freddie Mac (OTCQB: FMCC) today reported net income of $3.2 billion for the third quarter of 2026, up 17% from the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by higher net revenues and lower non-interest expense, partially offset by an increase in the provision for credit losses.
Net revenues were $6.8 billion for the third quarter of 2026, up 19% year-over-year, primarily driven by higher net interest income and higher non-interest income. Net interest income for the third quarter of 2026 was $5.9 billion, up 8% year-over-year, primarily driven by an increase in the balance of fully guaranteed securitizations in the Multifamily mortgage portfolio due to the change in Multifamily business strategy and continued mortgage portfolio growth in Single-Family. Non-interest income for the third quarter of 2026 was $0.9 billion, up from non-interest income of $0.3 billion in the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by net investment gains in the third quarter of 2026 compared to net investment losses in the third quarter of 2025.
The provision for credit losses was $0.8 billion for the third quarter of 2026, primarily due to a credit reserve build in Single-Family driven by an increase in projected future mortgage interest rates, as well as new acquisitions. The provision for credit losses was $0.2 billion for the third quarter of 2025, primarily driven by a credit reserve build in Single-Family attributable to new acquisitions.
Non-interest expense was $2.0 billion for the third quarter of 2026, down 6% year-over-year, primarily driven by lower credit enhancement expense, as well as a decrease in salaries and employee benefits.

About Freddie Mac’s Conservatorship
Since September 2008, Freddie Mac has been operating under conservatorship with FHFA as Conservator. The support provided by Treasury pursuant to the Purchase Agreement enables the company to maintain access to the debt markets and have adequate liquidity to conduct its normal business operations. The amount of funding available to Freddie Mac under the Purchase Agreement was $140.2 billion at September 30, 2026.
Pursuant to the Purchase Agreement, Freddie Mac will not be required to pay a dividend to Treasury on the senior preferred stock until it has built sufficient capital to meet the capital requirements and buffers set forth in the Enterprise Regulatory Capital Framework. As a result, the company was not required to pay a dividend to Treasury on the senior preferred stock in September 2026. As the company builds capital during this period, the quarterly increases in its Net Worth Amount have been, or will be, added to the aggregate liquidation preference of the senior preferred stock. The liquidation preference of the senior preferred stock increased to $150.4 billion on September 30, 2026 based on the increase in the Net Worth Amount during the second quarter of 2026, and will increase to $153.6 billion on December 31, 2026 based on the increase in the Net Worth Amount during the third quarter of 2026.
