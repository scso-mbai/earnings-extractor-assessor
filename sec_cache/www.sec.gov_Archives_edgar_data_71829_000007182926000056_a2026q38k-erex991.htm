MANAGEMENT COMMENTARY
“We generated third quarter year-over-year revenue and adjusted EBITDA growth of 11% and 30%, respectively, despite delayed deployments on several large projects,” stated Matthew Lanigan, President and CEO of NPK International. “While the exceptionally dry weather in Texas negatively impacted matting demand during the third and early fourth quarter and caused us to lower our full-year guidance, the underlying demand trends in our core power transmission markets remain robust as rental project activity has significantly strengthened throughout October. As a result, we expect strong rental and service trends to finish the year and our growth expectations for 2027 remain unchanged.
Lanigan continued, “We remain focused on executing our key growth initiatives, including expanding our manufacturing capacity, growing our rental fleet, and further diversifying our customer base and geographic footprint. Our manufacturing facility expansion remains on track to begin operations by mid-2027, supporting accelerated fleet growth and reducing our reliance on cross-rental mats. We also continue to evaluate organic growth opportunities and strategic acquisitions to expand our presence in new and underpenetrated markets and further increase market share. These investments reflect our confidence in the strength and long-term durability of the growth trends driving our business.
1

“Our strong liquidity position provides us the flexibility to balance our growth investments with the return of capital through our disciplined share repurchase program. During the third quarter, we invested $13 million in share repurchases, leaving $76 million remaining under our current share repurchase authorization. With debt of $26 million at quarter end and roughly $132 million of availability under our bank facility, we have ample financial capacity to support both our strategic growth investments, as well as opportunistic share repurchases.
“Our quoting activity has remained robust, maintaining year-over-year growth of approximately 20% throughout the year, demonstrating the strong fundamental demand trends benefitting our business. We believe our strategic investments position us well to capitalize on these attractive growth opportunities, which combined with our consistent operational execution, give us confidence in our ability to deliver double-digit rental growth in the coming years. We are excited about the opportunities ahead and remain focused on capitalizing on favorable market trends, executing our growth strategy and delivering long-term value for our shareholders,” concluded Lanigan
BUSINESS UPDATE
NPK’s business plan is designed to drive organic commercial growth within targeted, higher-margin product and rental markets; improve asset optimization and organizational efficiency; and pursue a capital allocation strategy that prioritizes investments with superior return profiles, together with a programmatic return of capital program.
Third quarter 2026 highlights include:
•Consistent customer demand from core markets. Business activity remains robust in core markets, driven by sustained demand from power transmission customers. Quoting activity remains consistent at elevated levels, with year-over-year growth of at least 20% throughout 2026, with much of the volume being originated in expansion geographies. This supports the Company’s longer-term expectation for double-digit rental growth.
•Improved operating efficiency. NPK remains focused on efficiency improvements and operating cost optimization across every aspect of its business. In the third quarter of 2026, NPK’s Adjusted EBITDA Margin was 26.2%, a 390 basis point improvement from the prior year period.
•Manufacturing capacity expansion. Based on the continued favorable long-term demand outlook, NPK continues to execute on plans to expand our Carencro, Louisiana manufacturing capacity by approximately 50% from current levels. The Company expects to invest $40 million to $45 million by mid-2027, including $10.5 million invested through September 30, 2026, with additional production expected to start up by mid-2027.
•Robust return of capital program. During the third quarter of 2026, the Company used $13.4 million of cash to repurchase 1.0 million outstanding shares under the repurchase program. The Company has $76 million remaining under the current share repurchase authorization.
FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE
In the third quarter of 2026, NPK generated revenue of $76.3 million, an increase of 11%, compared to $68.8 million in the prior year period. Rental and service revenue increased 12% to $49.6 million, while product sales increased 9% to $26.7 million. Rental demand in the third quarter of 2026 was impacted by an exceptionally dry weather pattern in Texas, which resulted in several scheduled large-scale utility projects commencing without the use of matting. The revenue decline in the Texas region was partially offset by growth in other targeted expansion markets.
Gross margin was 31.3% in the third quarter of 2026, compared to 31.9% in the prior year period.
2

Selling, general and administrative expenses were $14.7 million (19.3% of revenues) in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $13.3 million (19.3% of revenues) in the third quarter of 2025. SG&A for the third quarter of 2026 includes approximately $1.2 million in severance and related costs associated with executive leadership transition, as well as $0.7 million in strategic planning and diligence costs.
NPK generated income from continuing operations of $6.3 million, or $0.07 per diluted share, compared to $6.1 million, or $0.07 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 adjusted income from continuing operations was $7.9 million, or $0.09 per diluted share, compared to adjusted income from continuing operations of $6.1 million, or $0.07 per diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025.
The Company reported Adjusted EBITDA from Continuing Operations of $20.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, or 26.2% of total revenue, compared to $15.4 million, or 22.3% of total revenue, in the prior year period.
BALANCE SHEET AND LIQUIDITY
As of September 30, 2026, NPK had total cash of $4.2 million, total debt of $26.3 million, and available liquidity under its senior secured revolving credit facility of $132.2 million.
Operating cash flow was $7.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, and capital investments used $11.4 million, net, with the substantial majority funding the growth of the mat rental fleet and the manufacturing expansion project.

ABOUT NPK INTERNATIONAL
NPK International Inc. is a worksite access solutions company that manufactures, sells, and rents recyclable composite matting products, along with a full suite of services, including planning, logistics, and site restoration. The Company delivers superior quality and reliability across critical infrastructure markets, including electrical transmission and distribution, oil and gas exploration, pipeline, renewable energy, petrochemical, construction, and other industries. For more information, visit our website at npki.com.
