Nephros Announces Strong Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Year Over Year Net Revenue Growth of 18% and Core Programmatic Revenue Growth of 8% for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026.

 

SOUTH ORANGE, NJ, November 5, 2026 – Nephros, Inc. (Nasdaq: NEPH), a leading water technology company providing filtration solutions to the medical and commercial markets, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Financial Highlights

 

●Net revenue increased 18% to $5.6 million, compared to $4.8 million in the third quarter of 2025
●Net income was $0.5 million, compared to net income of $0.3 million in the third quarter of 2025
●Adjusted EBITDA was $0.6 million, compared to $0.4 million in the third quarter of 2025

 

“The third quarter of 2026 continued the strong momentum we have built across Nephros,” said Robert Banks, President and Chief Executive Officer of Nephros. “Net revenue increased approximately 18% over the prior-year quarter to $5.6 million, following the record $6.0 million we reported in the second quarter. Importantly, we continued to translate that growth into improved profitability, generating net income of approximately $0.5 million and Adjusted EBITDA of approximately $0.6 million. Our performance through the first nine months of the year reinforces our confidence in the underlying strength and durability of the business.”

 

Banks continued, “One of the most encouraging indicators during the quarter was the continued expansion of our customer base. We added approximately 31 new customer sites in the third quarter, bringing our active installed base to a record 1,766 sites. Healthcare remains at the core of Nephros, representing approximately 64% of new sites added during the quarter, while approximately 17% came from commercial and industrial customers and the remainder represented a diverse mix of government, military, hospitality, residential, retail and other applications. This breadth is important. It demonstrates that the value of our filtration technology is increasingly being recognized across applications beyond our traditional healthcare markets while we further expand our presence within healthcare. We continue to leverage emerging industry standards, regulatory developments, and market intelligence to identify unmet needs, inform strategic priorities, and support expansion into new applications and markets.”

 

Banks added, “We are also seeing our strategy evolve beyond products alone. During the quarter, the Nephros Water Institute continued a strong series of educational webinars, strengthening what we believe is becoming an important third pillar of our business alongside products and service. Education allows us to engage customers and industry professionals around emerging water-quality challenges, share the expertise we have developed over many years, and build relationships well before a filtration decision is made. We plan to deepen our investment in education as a driver of commercial growth and market development. We believe educational programming increases awareness of water safety challenges and available solutions, generates qualified leads, and sustains engagement with prospective customers throughout the sales cycle, supporting conversion and product adoption. We further believe the combination of differentiated products, service capabilities and education increasingly distinguishes Nephros in the markets we serve.”

 

Banks concluded, “As we enter the final quarter of 2026, our focus remains on execution. We have grown trailing twelve-month revenue to more than $21 million, expanded our active customer base, remained profitable, and continued investing in the products, services and capabilities that we believe can support the next stage of growth. Competition continues to evolve and the broader economic environment remains uncertain, but we believe Nephros is entering the final months of the year from a position of strength. Our objective is simple: finish 2026 strong and continue building a larger, more predictable and more valuable company for our shareholders.”

Financial Performance for the Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

 

Net revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026, and 2025 was $5.6 million and $4.8 million, respectively, an increase of 18%. This increase was driven primarily by increased product revenue from programmatic growth, which grew by 8% over the same period in 2025 as well as significant growth in emergency response revenue. In addition, the company reported growth in service revenue.

 

Cost of goods sold for the third quarter of 2026 was $2.3 million, compared with $1.8 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 26%.

 

Gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 58%, compared with 61% in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease of approximately three percentage points was primarily attributable to higher inventory shipping and handling costs, including tariff expense, and growth in service revenue, which yields lower gross margins than product sales.

 

Selling, general and administrative expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were approximately $2.4 million, compared with $2.2 million in 2025, an increase of 9% due to increases in headcount and an increase in professional fees.

 

Research and development expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were approximately $331,000, compared with $338,000 in the third quarter of 2025, a decrease of 2%.

 

Depreciation and amortization expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were approximately $29,000, compared with approximately $34,000 in the third quarter of 2025.

 

Net income for the third quarter of 2026 increased to $0.5 million, compared with $0.3 million during the same period in 2025.

 

Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter 2026 increased to approximately $0.6 million, compared with approximately $0.4 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

Financial Performance for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

 

Net revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, and 2025 was $16.9 million, and $14.1 million respectively, an increase of 20%. Core programmatic revenue grew by 19% over the same period in 2025. The increase in programmatic sales reflects strong reorders, and a number of new active sites. In addition, service revenue experienced strong growth.

 

Cost of goods sold for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 was $6.5 million, and $5.2 million respectively, an increase of 26%. Gross margin for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was 61%, compared with 63% during the same period in 2025. The decrease was primarily attributable to increased product costs due to the weakening of the U.S. dollar compared to the Euro, and rapid revenue growth from commercial products offerings and services revenue, both of which yield lower gross margins than the infection control business. However, gross margins significantly benefited from the recognition during the 2026 period of the IEEPA tariff refund of approximately $0.6 million, which was recorded as a reduction of cost of goods sold during the nine months ended September 30, 2026. This refund reflects tariffs paid by Nephros from April 2025 to February 2026. Approximately $0.3 million of this tariff refund corresponds to purchased inventory that was converted to revenue in 2026. The remaining approximately $0.3 million of the refunded tariffs correspond to purchased inventory that was converted to revenue in 2025, of which approximately $0.2 million corresponds to purchased inventory that was converted to revenue in the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

Selling, general and administrative expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 were approximately $7.4 million, and $6.7 million respectively, an increase of 10% primarily due to an increase in headcount and an increase in professional fees.

 

Research and development expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025 were $1.0 million and $0.9 million respectively.

Nephros believes that Adjusted EBITDA provides useful information to management and investors regarding certain financial and business trends relating to Nephros’ financial condition and results of operations. Management does not consider Adjusted EBITDA in isolation or as an alternative to financial measures determined in accordance with GAAP. The principal limitation of Adjusted EBITDA is that it excludes significant expenses and income that are required by GAAP to be recognized in Nephros’ financial statements. In addition, Adjusted EBITDA is subject to inherent limitations as it reflects the exercise of judgments by management about which expenses and income are excluded or included in determining Adjusted EBITDA. To compensate for these limitations, management presents Adjusted EBITDA in connection with net income, the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure. Nephros urges investors to review the reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA to net income and not to rely on any single financial measure to evaluate the business.
