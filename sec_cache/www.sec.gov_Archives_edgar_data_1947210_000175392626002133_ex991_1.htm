EXHIBIT 99.1

 

High Roller Technologies Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Highlights Commercial Launch of ROLR™ Prediction Markets Platform

 

         ROLR™ prediction markets platform launched to the public on October 21 and began generating revenue in the fourth quarter; the ROLR iOS app has been available in the Apple App Store since November 9

         No outstanding borrowings and $13.4 million in cash and cash equivalents at September 30, 2026

         Conference call today, November 13, at 4:30 p.m. ET

LAS VEGAS, Nevada, November 13, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- High Roller Technologies, Inc. (“High Roller” or the “Company”) (NYSE American: ROLR), a prediction markets and online gaming company, today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided an update on the commercial launch and continued development of its ROLR™ prediction markets business. High Roller will host a conference call today at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) to discuss third quarter results and provide a business update.

Management Commentary

“On October 21, our ROLR prediction markets platform went live to the public, and High Roller formally entered its next chapter,” said Seth Young, Chief Executive Officer of High Roller Technologies. “The ROLR iOS app launched in the Apple App Store on November 9, and we plan to release our Android app in Q4 2026. We believe prediction markets have the potential to become the major growth driver for High Roller in 2027.”

“Now our job is to bring customers onto the platform, learn from every trade, keep improving the product and put our distribution partners to work,” continued Young. “Since High Roller was founded in 2021, we believe the Company has demonstrated a track record of generating a positive return on customer acquisition ad spend across the markets and verticals it has operated in. We control our product roadmap, we’ve added proven leaders on product and partnerships, and we have another new product release planned before the end of November, among other strategic initiatives. Meanwhile, our team has managed the online casino business with real discipline through a deliberate strategic shift, and we expect Q3 2026 to be the last quarter in which prediction markets appear only on the expense side of the ledger. We’re confident in our playbook, and we’re playing to win.”

“Our Q3 2026 results reflect lower online casino revenue due to the year-over-year impact of having exited certain markets in 2025, as well as our continued investment to bring ROLR to market,” said Adam Felman, Chief Financial Officer of High Roller Technologies. “The casino customers we serve today are more valuable, with revenue per depositing customer more than doubling year over year. We ended the quarter with $13.4 million in cash and cash equivalents and no outstanding borrowings, which we believe is sufficient to fund our operations for at least the next twelve months.”

 


Prediction Markets Industry Outlook and Strategic Overview

Prediction markets bring together participants trading on outcomes across sports, financial markets, politics, entertainment and other real-world events. The industry has expanded rapidly: in a September 2026 note reported by The Block, Bernstein analysts estimated 2026 prediction market trading volume of approximately $410 billion and projected that annual volume could reach $10 trillion by 2035.1

High Roller’s strategy is to capture share of this growing market by combining access to event contracts offered through regulated exchange infrastructure with a consumer experience the Company controls, relevant market information and established digital distribution relationships. Management believes product quality, effective customer acquisition and retention, and responsible, compliant participation will be central to building the ROLR business.

Recent Strategic and Corporate Highlights

Third Quarter Developments

         Launched the invitation-only ROLR beta on September 15 to test the customer experience and operating infrastructure ahead of commercial availability.

         On August 13, entered into a platform and source code license and development agreement with DeepEther Labs, Ltd., the company behind mrkts.com, securing technology rights, dedicated development resources and ownership of the customer-facing ROLR application.

         Appointed Ben Sutherland as Senior Vice President, Product and Platform. Mr. Sutherland previously served as CEO of Greentube USA, the U.S. arm of NOVOMATIC’s digital gaming group, where he led U.S. operations and P&L across a multi-jurisdictional regulated footprint. Previously, Mr. Sutherland co-founded and managed Present Creative, an international game studio that produced more than 100 titles and live releases for partners including Disney, EA, Zynga, and Microsoft. NOVOMATIC’s Greentube acquired Present Creative in 2022.

Developments After Third Quarter End

         Commercially launched ROLR on October 21 for eligible U.S. customers. ROLR US LLC operates as a registered guaranteed introducing broker; customer accounts are carried by Crypto.com FCM.

         Appointed Flip Pidot, CFA, as Chief Business Officer, as announced October 13, to lead business development and strategic partnerships.

         Highlighted the ROLR Election Tracker at elections.rolr.com on November 2, featuring prediction market odds, interactive race maps and congressional-control indicators for the 2026 U.S. midterms.

         Supported the commercial rollout through marketing relationships with Lines.com, Forever Network, Leverage Game Media and the Company’s affiliated marketing partner Spike Up Media, alongside participation in industry and investor conferences.

         Registered with the National Futures Association as a commodity trading advisor, ahead of a new product release planned for later in November.

         Launched the ROLR iOS app in the Apple App Store on November 9, giving eligible customers mobile access to market discovery, trading and account management. An Android app is planned for release through Google Play before year-end.

         Announced on November 11 the formation of ROLR FCM LLC and the engagement of Connamara Technologies to advise the Company as it explores a potential application for registration as a Futures Commission Merchant (FCM) with the CFTC and NFA. Entity formation is a preliminary step; it is not an FCM application and does not authorize FCM operations, and there can be no assurance as to the timing or outcome of any registration.

         Announced on November 12 a commercial agreement with ProphetX intended to expand the range of event contracts available to eligible ROLR customers. The agreement becomes effective six months after ROLR FCM LLC receives FCM registration, unless the parties agree otherwise, and remains subject to integration and applicable regulatory requirements. The Company has not applied for FCM registration, and there can be no assurance that such registration will be obtained or that the agreement will become effective.

         Partnered with Kindbridge Behavioral Health to provide quarterly training for the Company’s customer-service team and a confidential pathway to care for eligible U.S.-based customers, including a planned Trader Care Fund to help eligible at-risk customers access treatment.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results Summary

         Net revenues were $2.4 million, down $2.5 million, or 51%, from $4.9 million in the third quarter of 2025, reflecting the year-over-year impact of markets exited and a revised marketing strategy. Prediction markets generated no third-quarter revenue.

         Average revenue per unique depositing customer was $507, more than double the $242 in the third quarter of 2025, reflecting the Company’s focus on higher-value players.

         Total operating expenses were $5.1 million, down $0.4 million, or 8%, from $5.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. Lower direct operating costs were partially offset by higher spending to prepare for the prediction markets launch.

         Loss from operations was $2.7 million, compared with a loss from operations of $0.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.

         Net loss from continuing operations was $2.8 million, or $(0.25) per basic and diluted share, versus income of $2.9 million, or $0.34 basic and $0.31 diluted, a year earlier. The prior-year quarter included a $3.6 million income tax benefit, primarily from the release of a valuation allowance on deferred tax assets of a Maltese subsidiary, versus $0.2 million of tax expense in the third quarter of 2026. Loss before income taxes was $2.6 million, compared with $0.8 million a year earlier.

         Total net loss was $2.8 million, or $(0.25) per basic and diluted share, versus income of $3.7 million, or $0.43 basic and $0.39 diluted, a year earlier. Prior-year income included $0.8 million from discontinued operations.

         Adjusted EBITDA was negative $2.1 million, versus negative $0.2 million a year earlier. See Non-GAAP Financial Measures below for reconciliations.

         Cash and cash equivalents excluding restricted cash were $13.4 million and stockholders’ equity was $27.3 million at September 30, 2026.

         In October 2026, the Company filed amended quarterly reports for the first and second quarters of 2026 to correct the accounting for approximately $1.0 million of non-cash placement-agent warrant costs. The amendment reduced previously reported net losses, and had no effect on revenue, cash, total stockholders’ equity or Adjusted EBITDA.

Additional information regarding the Company’s results of operations, financial condition and liquidity is included in its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, which the Company filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission on November 13, 2026.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

This press release includes Adjusted EBITDA, which is a non-GAAP financial measure that the Company uses to supplement its results presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. The Company believes this measure is useful in evaluating operating performance and trends and in making strategic decisions regarding the allocation of capital and new investments. This measure is not intended to be a substitute for any U.S. GAAP financial measure and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies.

The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net loss from continuing operations before the impact of interest income and expense, income tax provision or benefit, and depreciation and amortization, and further adjusted for the following items: stock-based compensation; and certain other costs or income, as described in the reconciliation below. The most directly comparable U.S. GAAP measure is net income (loss) from continuing operations.
