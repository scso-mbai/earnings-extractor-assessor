Kartoon Studios Reports Third Quarter 2026

Financial Results and Operational Highlights

 

Cash and Marketable Securities of $33.0 Million at September 30, 2026

 

Walmart Marketplace and Amazon Prime Agreements Expected to Propel Hundred Acre Wood

Streaming and Consumer Products Launch Beginning Q1 2027

 

BEVERLY HILLS, Calif. – November 13, 2026 -- Kartoon Studios, Inc. (NYSE American: TOON) (“Kartoon Studios” or the “Company”), a global entertainment company creating, producing, distributing and licensing children’s and family content, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided an update on its strategy to focus on the ownership, development and commercialization of high-value intellectual property assets for children.

 

Recent Operational Highlights

 

·Cash and marketable securities totaled $33.0 million at quarter-end, providing resources to advance the development and commercialization of Hundred Acre Wood and continue to strengthen the Balance Sheet. The Company’s capital allocation priorities center on disciplined investment in owned intellectual property and opportunities to generate revenue across content, licensing and consumer products.

 

·Advanced the launch and commercialization strategy for Hundred Acre Wood through complementary agreements with Walmart Marketplace and Amazon Prime, combining television and streaming distribution with licensed consumer products. Toys, books and apparel are expected on Walmart Marketplace beginning January 18, 2027, followed by the planned Amazon Prime series launch on February 18, 2027. Together, these agreements support audience development and opportunities to generate revenue across content distribution, licensing and consumer products.

 

·Strengthened audience engagement across Kartoon Channel! and Ameba SVOD, as total hours watched more than doubled year-over-year through the first three quarters of 2026. These owned platforms support the Company’s strategy by generating subscription and advertising revenue while providing channels to introduce, promote and build audiences for its intellectual property. Kartoon Studios, Kartoon Channel streaming service, continues to rank #1 in the Apple app store, above all the major streamers including Netflix, You Tube, Disney+, PBS Kids, and others.*

 

·Refocused the Company’s portfolio through the sale of the Frederator channel network business while retaining Frederator Studios intellectual property, including Bee & Puppycat, Bravest Warriors, and Catbug. The transaction advances the shift toward owning and commercializing content and characters with opportunities for long-term franchise development, licensing and consumer products revenue.

 

Management Commentary

 

“We are building a fundamentally different Kartoon Studios, with ownership of great intellectual property at the center of our business and multiple ways to turn that creativity into lasting shareholder value,” said Andy Heyward, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer. “Our agreements with Amazon Prime and Walmart Marketplace bring that strategy to life, connecting entertainment with opportunities for families to discover and purchase products featuring our characters. That is what makes this opportunity so exciting. We can build an audience on screen and extend that connection into toys, books, apparel and much more.”

Hundred Acre Wood

 

Hundred Acre Wood, based on characters and stories created by A.A. Milne, is central to Kartoon Studios’ strategy to build owned intellectual property into enduring family friendly safe franchises, that generate revenue across entertainment, licensing and consumer products. The Company’s agreements with Walmart Marketplace and Amazon Prime support complementary parts of that strategy, pairing consumer products availability with audience development through streaming and opportunities to translate engagement into purchases of products featuring the characters.

 

Licensed toys, books, apparel and other products are expected to be available on Walmart Marketplace beginning January 18, 2027, followed by the planned Amazon Prime series launch on February 18, 2027. The recently announced Amazon Prime agreement includes promotional support and participation in Amazon’s Shop the Show program, helping audiences discover Hundred Acre Wood and connecting the viewing experience with shopping opportunities. Together, these arrangements establish distribution and commerce channels that support the property’s launch while creating opportunities for licensing and royalty revenue beyond the screen.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

The third-quarter results primarily reflect the Company’s historical operating model and do not yet reflect the anticipated contribution from its evolving intellectual property strategy, including the planned launch and commercialization of Hundred Acre Wood. Results also reflect the July 8, 2026 sale of Frederator Networks, Inc., with the sold business excluded from consolidated results after that date.

 

Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $5.4 million, compared with $9.9 million (which included $1.3 million from the Frederator channel network business) in the prior-year period. Production services revenue decreased 53% to $3.3 million, primarily reflecting the timing and stage of production activity at Mainframe Studios. Content distribution revenue decreased 67% to $0.6 million, primarily due to the sale of the Frederator channel network business, partially offset by higher subscription and advertising revenue at Ameba and Kartoon Channel! Media advisory and advertising services revenue increased 51% to $1.4 million, driven by increased demand and an expanded scope of campaigns from existing customers.

 

Total operating expenses decreased 29% to $9.1 million, compared with $12.8 million in the prior-year period. Direct operating costs declined 57% to $3.0 million, primarily reflecting lower production-related headcount and the sale of Frederator Networks. General and administrative expenses decreased 10% to $5.1 million, reflecting lower salaries and administrative costs, partially offset by higher professional fees. Marketing and sales expenses were $0.1 million, compared with $0.2 million. Operating expenses also included a $0.9 million non-cash impairment charge related to the WOW Unlimited Media trade names, reflecting lower projected revenue.

 

Other expense, net, decreased to $1.1 million from $3.5 million in the prior-year period, primarily reflecting investment valuation losses, sale of Frederator Networks, and legal settlement fees, partially offset by interest income and foreign exchange gains.

 

Loss from Operations was $3.7 million (or a loss of $2.8 million when removing the loss impact from one-time impairment of intangible assets) million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $2.9 million in the prior-year period. Net loss attributable to Kartoon Studios, Inc. was $5.7 million, or $0.08 per basic and diluted share, compared with a net loss of $6.6 million, or $0.14 per basic and diluted share, in the prior-year period.

 

Cash and marketable securities totaled $33.0 million at September 30, 2026, compared with $40.5 million at June 30, 2026. The $7.5 million decrease included a $4.0 million payment in July under a previously disclosed standstill and voting agreement entered into in connection with the litigation settlements, plus additional legal expenses related to litigation settlement.

 

The remaining approximately $39.2 million of litigation settlement proceeds held in escrow is excluded from these balances; any residual amount payable to the Company remains subject to court-awarded legal fees and expenses and final approval orders.

 

About Kartoon Studios

 

Kartoon Studios (NYSE American: TOON) is a global, vertically integrated children’s and family entertainment company turning owned and controlled intellectual property into enduring, multi-platform franchises. The Company develops, produces, distributes, licenses and monetizes content across the full value chain, creating multiple revenue opportunities and long-term brand value.

 

Kartoon Studios’ growth portfolio includes Hundred Acre Wood and the Stan Lee Universe, alongside established brands and an extensive programming library. The Company operates Mainframe Studios and Toon Media Networks, as well as Beacon Media Group, a full-service marketing, communications, and media agency subsidiary of Kartoon Studios focused on children and family. Together, these assets provide production capabilities, direct audience access and distribution across linear television, AVOD, SVOD, FAST channels and streaming platforms in more than 60 territories. Kartoon Studios is focused on converting its intellectual property, infrastructure and global reach into scalable franchise growth and long-term shareholder value.
